Profile

Yan Diomande is a 19-year-old Ivorian-French winger born on 14 November 2006 in Abidjan, Ivory Coast. 180cm, right-footed, primarily a right winger who can also operate on the left or as a second striker. Moved to the United States at age 15, joining DME Academy in 2022 and winning the 2023 STARI Player of the Year. Brief trial at Rangers (Oct 2023) did not result in a contract. Signed with CD Leganés in November 2024, making his La Liga debut in March 2025 and scoring his first professional goal in May 2025. On 16 July 2025, RB Leipzig activated his €20M release clause for a five-year deal. In his debut Bundesliga season (2025-26), he exploded with 12 goals and 8 assists in 33 appearances, winning Bundesliga Rookie of the Season and VDV Newcomer of the Season. On 27 July 2026, Real Madrid agreed a €120M deal for his transfer. Agent: Roc Nation Sports.
