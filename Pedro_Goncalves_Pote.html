Full Scouting Report
Jim Totime · Empire FGA · Apr 2026
Pedro Gonçalves is a technical, left‑footed attacking midfielder (plays right-footed) who excels in possession‑based systems. His diagonal passing (7.71) and set‑piece delivery are elite – ranking in the top 3% of Primeira Liga. He consistently arrives late in the box to score (10 goals in 17 apps). However, physical limitations (pace, strength) and inconsistent off‑ball movement limit his impact against elite opposition.
The video log confirms excellent decision‑making (DMA 8.2) and creative risk‑taking (CRK 8.0), but OBI (off‑ball intelligence) remains unassessed. His defensive work rate (DEF 3.37) is below average – requires a protective midfielder.
