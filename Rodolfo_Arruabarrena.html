Profile

Rodolfo Martín Arruabarrena is a 21-year-old Argentine left-back born on 20 July 1975 in Marcos Paz, Buenos Aires. 177cm, 75kg. This assessment covers his Clausura 1997 season at Boca Juniors, where he won the left-back starting spot over Pineda through sheer work rate, making 12 appearances. He had previously spent Apertura '96 on loan at Rosario Central (15 appearances). A youth international for Argentina under Reinaldo Merlo, he played in the 1991 South American U-17 championship alongside Gallardo and Husain (Argentina finished runner-up) and the Viareggio U-20 tournament (3rd place).
