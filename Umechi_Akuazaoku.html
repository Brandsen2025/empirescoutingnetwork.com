Profile

Umechi Daniel Akuazaoku is a 22-year-old Canadian striker of Nigerian descent, born in Toronto, Ontario on 10 June 2003. At 190cm (6'3") and ~86kg, he is a right-footed, physically imposing centre-forward with a strong academic and athletic background. Began at the Dwayne DeRosario Football Academy (DERO FA), where he was the top scorer in 2018-19 with 25 goals. Also a member of the Biosteel Canadian U16 Select Team and selected for Team Ontario Trials. Attended Bill Crothers Secondary School and played varsity for Donald A Wilson Gators for 3 years. Played a summer season with FC Buffalo in 2022. Four-year college career at St. Bonaventure University (Atlantic 10 Conference): 58 games, 36 starts, 14 goals, 5 assists, 33 points, 5 game-winning goals. Signed his first professional contract with Sakaryaspor (TFF 1. Lig, Turkey) on 7 August 2025. Contract until 30 June 2027. Agent: 8SPORTSAGENT. Marketing major. Atlantic 10 Commissioner's Honor Roll. Son of Urenna and Valentine Akuazaoku. Siblings: Ugochi and Udochi.
