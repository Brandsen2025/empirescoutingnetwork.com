Born in Terrassa, Catalonia, Spain — the same region that produced Xavi, Iniesta, and Pep Guardiola. Early childhood exposure to Spanish technical football culture before family relocation to Belgium.
2011-2019 — KRC Genk (Youth Development)
Belgian Pro League academy. Developed through KRC Genk's renowned youth system (producers of De Bruyne, Courtois, Benteke). Technical foundation built on Belgian school methodology: positional flexibility, technical security, and tactical intelligence. Dual-nationality pathway opened options for Belgium or Morocco senior teams.
2020-2022 — PSV Eindhoven (Academy & Transition)
Dutch transition. Moved to PSV Eindhoven from Genk in 2020, adapting from Belgian Pro League physicality to Eredivisie technical demands. 2022-2024 — Breakthrough & International Debut
Eredivisie breakthrough. Established as PSV rotation player, contributing 8 goals and 6 assists in 2023-24. Morocco national team debut (September 2023), choosing Atlas Lions over Belgium despite Belgian youth nationality. Featured in 2023 Africa Cup of Nations reaching round of 16.
2024-Present — Elite Ascension
Established starter. 2024-25 season: key role in PSV's Champions League campaign (round of 16 appearance). 2025 Africa Cup of Nations Final with Morocco (runners-up). Current 2025-26 season: 11 goals in 19 Eredivisie matches — elite attacking output. FGA Rating 7.7 confirms top-5 league potential.
