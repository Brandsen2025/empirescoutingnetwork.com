Profile

Christian Frederick Bayoi Makoun Reyes is a 26-year-old Venezuelan centre-back/defensive midfielder born on 5 March 2000 in Valencia, Carabobo, Venezuela. A product of the Zamora FC academy (joined 2010 at age 10), he made his professional debut in 2016 and spent three seasons with the club (32 apps, 1 goal), winning the Copa Venezuela 2019 and Primera Division 2016. In 2018-19, he had a loan spell at Juventus U23/Primavera (21 apps, 3 goals). In 2019, he joined Inter Miami CF as their third-ever signing (30 MLS apps, 2 goals across 2020-21), with a brief loan to Fort Lauderdale CF (3 apps). He was traded to Charlotte FC in January 2022 (13 apps), then to New England Revolution in August 2022 (17 apps). In January 2024, he returned to Europe with Anorthosis Famagusta in Cyprus (13 apps, 2 goals). On 7 August 2024, he signed a three-year contract with Levski Sofia in Bulgaria, where he has become a key player — 42+ apps, 4 goals, and won the Bulgarian First League 2025-26, ending Ludogorets' dominance. On the international stage, he has 15 caps for Venezuela (2022-2025), was part of the 2024 Copa America squad, and was a FIFA U-20 World Cup runner-up in 2017. He also holds Belgian citizenship through his Cameroonian father, a former professional who played for Cercle Brugge.
