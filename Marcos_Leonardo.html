Career Timeline

Born May 2, 2003 · Itapetinga, Bahia, Brazil
Born in Itapetinga, Bahia, Brazil. Father Marcos Coringa was also a footballer (forward, amateur). Moved to Taubaté, São Paulo in May 2014. Joined Santos youth setup in August 2014 after a trial.
Aug 2020 · Santos Professional Debut · vs Sport Recife
First professional appearance at 17. Substitute for Yeferson Soteldo in 1-0 away win. First goal on Oct 4 vs Goiás (3-2). Copa Libertadores debut vs Olimpia. Became sixth-youngest scorer in Libertadores history vs Defensa y Justicia (Oct 2020).
2021–2023 · Santos · Série A · 168 apps, 54G, 11A
"Menino da Vila" era. Became undisputed starter from 2022. Renewed contract until 2026. Entered Santos' top 50 all-time goalscorers after winner vs Palmeiras (Oct 2023). 2023: 49 apps, 21 goals — but Santos were relegated for the first time in history. 2023 · Brazil U20 World Cup · Argentina
Top scorer for Brazil. 5 goals in the tournament. Helped Brazil reach knockout stages. Established as one of the most promising Brazilian strikers of his generation.
Jan 2024 · Benfica · Primeira Liga · €18M
European debut. Signed five-and-a-half-year contract. Release clause: €150M. Scored in each of his first three league games (Rio Ave, Boavista, Estrela da Amadora). 14 league apps, 7 goals. Europa League debut vs Toulouse (won penalty for Di María). Taça de Portugal and Taça da Liga semi-finalist. Primeira Liga runner-up.
Sep 2024 · Al-Hilal · Saudi Pro League · €40M
The Saudi chapter. Five-year contract. Debut vs Al-Riyadh (3-0). First goal vs Al-Rayyan (3-1, AFC Champions League). Hat-trick vs Al-Fateh (9-0, Jan 2025) — penalty, long-range shot, header from Rúben Neves free-kick. Left injured. 79 total apps, 47 goals.
Jun 2025 · Club World Cup · Al-Hilal 4-3 Man City
The defining moment. Round of 16. Scored twice including the winning goal in extra-time as Al-Hilal defeated Manchester City 4-3. The goal that announced him to the global elite. "Al-Hilal strike twice in extra time" — The Guardian.
Feb 2026 · Atletico Madrid Loan Collapse
Almost Spain. Atletico Madrid agreed a loan with €40M option to buy. Leonardo wanted to impress Carlo Ancelotti before WC2026. Al-Hilal pulled the plug due to Karim Benzema transfer uncertainty.
2026 · Ajax · Eredivisie
The Amsterdam project. Moved to AFC Ajax for the 2026-27 season. Wears #9. The Eredivisie should suit his finishing — but the step down from Saudi wages and the pressure of replacing departed strikers will test his mentality.
