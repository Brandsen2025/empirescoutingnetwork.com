🌍 Empire FGA Analysis — The Serie A Graduate

Josip Posavec represents the archetype of the well-traveled professional goalkeeper — a player who has experienced the highest highs (Serie A with Palermo) and navigated the complexities of multiple leagues (Italy, Denmark, Slovenia, Croatia) before settling into his prime years. At 29 years old, he stands at the peak of his physical capabilities with nearly a decade of professional experience across four countries. Currently the first-choice goalkeeper for NK Lokomotiva Zagreb, he has found stability in the Croatian top flight after a challenging 2024/25 season marked by injury setbacks.

🌍 Career Journey — From Varaždin to Serie A and Back

Twelve-year professional odyssey across four countries and seven clubs.

2014-2016 — NK Inter Zaprešić
Professional debut at age 18 in Croatian First League (2. HNL). Promoted to first team in 2014/15, helping club win league title and achieve promotion. 20+ appearances in second tier demonstrated raw potential and shot-stopping ability. Attracted Italian scouts.
Jan 2016 — US Palermo (Serie A)
Sensational move to Italian top flight at age 19. Initially backup, earned starting spot in 2016/17 Serie A season. 30 Serie A appearances facing world-class attackers (Higuaín, Dybala, Mertens). Experienced relegation battle and Palermo's financial crisis. Mixed performances — brilliant saves but occasional errors.
2017-2019 — Palermo & Serie B
Relegation to Serie B. Continued as starter (22 apps) but club instability mounted. Contract disputes and financial issues. Loaned to Hajduk Split in July 2018 to regain form and playing time in more stable environment.
2018-2022 — Hajduk Split
Career peak. Initial loan (2018-19) made permanent 2019. Four seasons as first-choice. Won Croatian Cup 2021/22. Europa League qualifiers experience. 73 total appearances. Established as fan favorite with spectacular saves and commanding presence. Market value peaked €1.5M.
2022-2024 — AaB Aalborg (Denmark)
Move to Danish Superliga. 34 appearances across 18 months. Solid performances but relegation battle with AaB. Cultural and tactical adaptation to Northern European football. Contract terminated by mutual consent January 2024.
2024-2025 — HNK Rijeka
Returned to HNL with ambitious Rijeka. Backup role initially, limited to 2 league appearances. Loaned to ND Primorje (Slovenia) January 2025 for playing time. Injury disrupted spell (Oct-Dec 2024).
Jan-Jun 2025 — ND Primorje (Loan)
Slovenian PrvaLiga loan. 15 appearances, 1 clean sheet. Mixed results but regained match fitness. Returned to Croatia June 2025.
Jul 2025-Present — NK Lokomotiva
Free transfer to NK Lokomotiva Zagreb. Immediate first-choice status. Contract until 2027. Currently playing full HNL season with mixed results (2 clean sheets in 18 apps). Current Role
The Well-Traveled Professional: Posavec's career illustrates the challenges of modern goalkeeping — early promise in Italy disrupted by institutional instability, peak years in Croatia, then gradual decline through Nordic football and backup roles. His return to Lokomotiva at 29 represents a career reset — accepting a role at a smaller Zagreb club to ensure first-team football rather than warming benches at bigger clubs. The injury setbacks in 2024/25 (two separate spells) are concerning for a goalkeeper entering his 30s.
