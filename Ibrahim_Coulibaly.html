Full Scouting Report
Jim Totime · Empire FGA · Apr 2026
Ibrahim Coulibaly is a high-velocity transition right-back currently establishing himself in Ligue 2 with Clermont Foot. His Sprint Intensity Index (SII 8.51) ranks in the elite tier for full-backs, combining explosive acceleration (SII_A 8.0) with strong top-end speed (SII_T 8.75).
His Off-Ball Positional Anticipation (OBI_P 8.5) is his standout cognitive trait — consistently arriving in dangerous positions 2+ seconds before the ball, particularly in transition phases. The Omschakeling (OMS 8.67) score confirms exceptional speed in defensive-to-attacking transitions.
However, critical technical limitations emerge under pressure. The Speed on Action (SOA 6.36) score — the lowest validated metric — reveals consistent technical breakdowns at high velocity: "awful dribble attempts," poor ball adjustment when carrying at speed, and predictable passing patterns (noted by commentators). The event log shows 2 negative SOA events vs 7 positive, indicating flashes of quality but significant inconsistency.
Decision Making (DMA 8.5) shows paradoxical results — excellent crossing decisions (9.0 positive events) but predictable through-passes under pressure (anti-indicators noted). Suggests good vision but rushed execution when pressed.
Assessment Gaps: Character (CHR), Mental Competence (MCS), Spatial Performance (SPI), and Clutch Response (CRS) remain pending. These are critical for determining if he can step up to Ligue 1 pressure environments.
Empire FGA Final Verdict
Developmental Prospect — High Variance. Coulibaly offers elite physical tools for transition-based systems but requires significant technical refinement. Recommended for counter-attacking Ligue 1 sides or as a back-up wing-back in 3-5-2 systems. Not yet ready for possession-dominant top-tier clubs. Complete CHR/MCS assessment before major investment. Market value: €800k–1.5M (pending completion).
