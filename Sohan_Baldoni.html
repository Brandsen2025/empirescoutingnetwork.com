🌍 Empire FGA Analysis — The Provincial Poacher

Sohan Baldoni represents the quintessential French late-blooming striker — a player forged in the semi-professional depths of Championnat National 3 (5th tier) before gradually climbing the pyramid. At 22, he is only now experiencing his first true professional platform with US Concarneau in the National (3rd tier), having spent the first half of 2025/26 terrorizing National 2 defenses for Lorient's reserves (7 goals in 10 games). Standing at 1.87m with ambidextrous finishing ability, Baldoni profiles as a traditional target man with enough mobility to drift wide, embodying the "provincial poacher" archetype that French football continues to produce from its deep regional leagues.
