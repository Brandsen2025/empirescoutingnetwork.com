🌍 Empire FGA Analysis — The Cross Master

Jérémy Livolant represents the archetype of the modern inverted winger — a crossing specialist with exceptional dead-ball ability, high character, and remarkable consistency. His FGA rating of 6.16 places him in the High Quality tier — combining high crossing volume (115 crosses) with creative passing vision (7 assists) and outstanding mental stability (CHR 9.6, CLM 7.54). Playing for Casa Pia AC in Portugal's Primeira Liga, Livolant has established himself as one of the league's most reliable creative outlets. Unlike the flashy dribblers or pure goalscorers, Livolant is the complete cross specialist — delivering quality from wide areas with both feet, creating chances from set-pieces, and maintaining composure under pressure. His Breton roots (born in Brest) reflect the technical French footballing culture — intelligent movement, precise delivery, and tactical discipline.

Livolant operates as an inverted left winger in Casa Pia's 4-2-3-1 system. He drifts centrally to link play but his primary weapon is the diagonal cross to the back post. Defensively, he contributes 11 interceptions and 15 tackles — showing willingness to work without the ball. 🌍 Career Journey — The Breton Technician

Seven-year journey through French football's academies to Portuguese prominence.

2014-2018 — Stade Brestois
Rose through Brest academy — hometown club in Brittany. Made 23 Ligue 2 appearances as a teenager, showing early promise with set-piece delivery. Loaned to Guingamp (2017) for first-team experience. Technical foundation built in Breton footballing culture — emphasis on ball retention and tactical intelligence.
2018-2020 — EA Guingamp
First major breakthrough. 58 appearances across Ligue 1 and Ligue 2 as Guingamp yo-yoed between divisions. Developed crossing specialization under coaches Jocelyn Gourvennec and Patrice Lair. 5 goals and 8 assists — establishing himself as a creative winger rather than pure scorer. Won 2019 Coupe de la Ligue (though injured for final).
2020-2022 — FC Lorient
Ligue 1 consolidation. 62 appearances over two seasons, including top-flight survival battles. Worked under Christophe Pélissier — known for organized, defensive systems where Livolant learned defensive responsibilities. 3 goals, 5 assists — steady if unspectacular. Released in 2022 as Lorient financially restructured.
2022-2024 — EA Guingamp (Return)
Homecoming and peak. Returned to Guingamp as experienced Ligue 2 veteran. 65 appearances, 8 goals, 12 assists — his most productive period. Developed leadership qualities (CHR 9.6 reflects this). Became primary set-piece taker — corners, free-kicks, penalties. Cult hero status at Stade de Roudourou.
2024-Present — Casa Pia AC
Portuguese adventure. Joined Casa Pia on a free transfer (Summer 2024) — seeking new challenge outside France. Immediate impact: 22 starts in 22 games — ever-present. 7 assists place him top 5 in Primeira Liga. His crossing has become the primary attacking weapon for a mid-table side. Career renaissance at age 28 — proving late bloomer trajectory.
The Late Bloomer Pattern: Livolant's career follows the classic French wide player trajectory — academy product, early loans, Ligue 1 consolidation, then foreign adventure. Unlike the elite French wingers who explode early (Mbappé, Dembélé), Livolant has gradually improved through his 20s. His move to Portugal at 27 is strategic — seeking visibility in a league that exports to top 5 leagues. Scouting insight: Players with 115 crosses and 7 assists in mid-table teams are prime candidates for top club recruitment — they demonstrate consistent delivery despite limited attacking support.
