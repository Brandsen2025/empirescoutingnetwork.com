Profile

Diego Fernando Latorre (born 4 August 1969 in Buenos Aires) is an Argentine former professional footballer who played as a second striker or attacking midfielder. Nicknamed "Gambetita" (the little dribbler), he was one of the first Argentine players to be labelled the "New Maradona".[reference:0] He played for 12 clubs across Argentina, Italy, Spain, Mexico, and Guatemala over an 18-year career, making 304 appearances and scoring 94 goals.[reference:1] He earned 6 caps for Argentina, scoring 1 goal.[reference:2]

Tactical Archetype

"The Enganche — Argentine Playmaker" — Latorre was a classic Argentine enganche (attacking midfielder/playmaker), known for his dribbling, passing range, and ability to operate in tight spaces. He was the natural deputy to Maradona in the enganche role, also capable off either flank. Small but skillful, with a sharp passing range and a habit of getting into shooting positions from the wide role.
