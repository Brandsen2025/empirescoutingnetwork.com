Profile

San Lorenzo's back-up goalkeeper; only gets a run in the side through suspension or injury to the starter, Passet. Given a run of matches last season by Castelli after Passet's poor form, but the source says he still hasn't shown the club's fans or coaching staff enough to be considered a genuinely reliable no. 1 option. His only sustained run of games came when Passet suffered a serious shoulder injury; while he didn't make any major errors in that spell, the source felt he lacked the stature to be San Lorenzo's first-choice goalkeeper.

Career to date (as of mid-1997)

A league title (Clausura '95). His move to San Lorenzo opened the door to Venezuela's national team — he had never been called up while playing in Venezuela. Played the 1995 Copa América in Uruguay, where Venezuela performed poorly, though Angelucci himself was rated as one of his team's better players. Later recalled for France '98 qualifying, for a 2-0 home loss to Uruguay — his last international appearance per the source. Debuted in the Argentine Primera División on 29 November 1994 against Talleres de Córdoba, having arrived from Minervén of Venezuela; manager Veira had scouted him in two Copa Libertadores matches against Vélez Sarsfield and liked what he saw. Given four starts last season by Castelli due to Oscar Passet's poor form; acceptable performances, but not enough to convince the manager to extend his run. 4 appearances in Clausura '97.
