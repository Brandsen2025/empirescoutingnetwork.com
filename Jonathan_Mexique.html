🌍 Dual Identity — From Le Mans to Fort-de-France

French education, Martinique representation — the post-colonial football narrative.

1995-2013 — Le Mans, France
Born in Le Mans, Sarthe department. Parents from Saint-Esprit, Martinique. Joined Le Mans FC academy at age 11, developing alongside future stars Martial and Lemar. French footballing education emphasizing tactical discipline and technical security.
2013-2019 — Monaco Academy
6-year cycle at Monaco's elite academy. Promoted to first team in 2015 but never debuted. Multiple loan spells (Red Star, Cercle Brugge, Tours, Cholet) characterizing the "loan army" experience of elite academy products who don't break into first teams.
2019-2025 — National League Specialist
Found his professional level in Championnat National (3rd tier). Established himself as reliable box-to-box midfielder at Cholet (captain), Châteauroux, and Nîmes. Developed from technical prospect to combative, experienced pro.
2022 — International Switch
After 14 caps for France U16/U17, switched allegiance to Martinique (parents' homeland). Debut June 2022 vs Costa Rica. Scored first senior goal in 2023 Gold Cup vs Costa Rica (6-4 loss). Represents growing trend of French-Caribbean dual nationals selecting overseas departments for senior careers.
2025 — Sochaux Chapter
Joined historic FC Sochaux-Montbéliard, returning the club to its competitive roots in National division. Brings veteran leadership to promotion-chasing side.
