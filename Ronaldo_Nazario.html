Career Timeline

Born 22 Sep 1976
Born Luiz Nazário de Lima in Belo Horizonte, Brazil.
1993-94 · Cruzeiro
Professional debut at 16 with Cruzeiro. Scored 12 goals in 14 games. Included in Brazil's 1994 World Cup squad.
1994-95 · PSV
First European club. Sold for €6M. 30 goals in 33 Eredivisie matches. Revealed the full Ronaldo model to Europe.
1995-96 · PSV
Second season at PSV. Continued prolific form, but suffered a knee injury that would become a recurring theme.
1996-97 · FC Barcelona
THE ASSESSED SEASON. 34 goals in 37 La Liga matches. 5 hat-tricks. FIFA World Player of the Year. The matches covered span the full range of his La Liga campaign.
1997-2002 · Inter Milan
World record transfer to Inter (€27M). Devastating knee injuries in 1999 and 2000 cost three years.
2002 · Brazil · World Cup
8 goals · 2 in the final · Golden Boot · World Cup winner. The pre-injury Ronaldo of 1996-97 and the post-injury Ronaldo of 2002 are the same player at different physical states.
2002-07 · Real Madrid
Post-injury · 177 appearances, 104 goals · 2× La Liga · Galáctico.
2007-08 · AC Milan
Loan to Milan. Suffered a serious knee injury in February 2008.
2008-09 · AC Milan
Recovery season; limited appearances due to persistent physical issues.
2009-11 · Corinthians
Return to Brazil. Scored 35 goals in 69 games. Retired in February 2011.
