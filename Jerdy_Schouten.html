Career Timeline
Born Jan 12, 1997 · Hellevoetsluis, South Holland
Football family. Great-uncle Henk Schouten played for Excelsior and Feyenoord and won 3 Netherlands caps in the 1950s. Joined the ADO Den Haag academy at age 11; a compartment syndrome injury at 17 required two surgeries and nearly a year out.
Dec 2016 · ADO Den Haag · Eredivisie debut
Limited breakthrough. Debut 10 Dec 2016 vs N.E.C. Released in 2017 after 3 total appearances amid a coaching change and stiff midfield competition.
2017-18 · Telstar · Eerste Divisie
Attacking-mid breakout. Free transfer; 37 league apps, 4 goals, 2 assists as an attacking midfielder — the opposite role to the one that later defined his career.
2018-19 · Excelsior · Eredivisie · Player of the Year
The positional switch. Converted fully to defensive midfield. 31 league apps, voted club Player of the Year by fans — the form that took him to Serie A.
Jul 2019 · Bologna FC · Serie A · ~€2-3M
Four years in Italy. 109 apps, 2 goals across four Serie A seasons (2019-23). Passing accuracy climbed from 82.4% to a career-high 86.7% (2022-23) as he matured into a deep-lying playmaker under successive coaches.
Jun 2022 · Netherlands senior debut
Full international. Debut 8 Jun 2022 vs Wales (Nations League), played 67 minutes, assisted Teun Koopmeiners' opener in a 2-1 win.
Aug 2023 · PSV Eindhoven · ~€12M · 5-year deal
Repatriation. Returned to the Eredivisie on 16 Aug 2023. Immediate impact: 40 total apps, 4 goals as PSV won the 2023-24 Eredivisie — their first title in six years.
Jun-Jul 2024 · UEFA Euro 2024 · Netherlands
"Unsung hero." Started all 6 Dutch matches (Group + Round of 16 + QF + SF) after Frenkie de Jong's injury withdrawal. Netherlands reached the semi-final (lost 1-2 to England) — their best run since 2004. Daily Mail profile: "Netherlands' unsung hero."
2024-25 · PSV back-to-back champions
Title defended. 30 total apps, 2 goals as PSV won a 26th Eredivisie title, Schouten a regular alongside Joey Veerman in central midfield.
26 Jul 2025 · Named PSV captain
Succeeds Luuk de Jong. "I'm not going to be Luuk de Jong 2.0 ... I'll be the captain that I am as a person." New contract to Jun 30, 2030 signed 6 Aug 2025.
3 Aug 2025 · Johan Cruyff Shield winners
First trophy as captain. 2-1 comeback win over Go Ahead Eagles — PSV's 15th Super Cup.
2025-26 · Eredivisie 3-peat · UCL league phase
Record 27th title. 28 starts, 2,381 Eredivisie minutes, 7.37 average rating, 8 UCL appearances. PSV clinched the title on 5 April 2026 — one day after Schouten's injury.
4 Apr 2026 · Severe ACL injury vs FC Utrecht
Season-ending, World Cup-ending. Twisted his knee in the 65th minute of a 4-3 win. Surgery required; 6-9 month recovery rules him out of the rest of 2025-26 and the 2026 FIFA World Cup.
