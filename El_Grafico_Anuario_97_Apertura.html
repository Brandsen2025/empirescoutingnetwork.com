This is a standalone reference page, not part of the graded player database. Every figure below is transcribed directly from two pages of a scanned magazine issue — El Gráfico #4082 (30 December 1997), the "Anuario '97" year-end supplement, pages 24 ("El Equipo del Apertura") and 26 ("El Ranking del Apertura" / "Los Más y Los Menos"). Nothing here has been inferred, estimated, or cross-filled — where a name is abbreviated on the page (e.g. "S.A. Berti", "C.R. Ayala"), it's reproduced exactly as abbreviated rather than expanded to a guessed full name.
Torneo Apertura 1997 — a different half-season from the Clausura '97 data elsewhere on this platform. Argentina's 1990s Primera División split each year into two independent short tournaments: Clausura (played first calendar-year, roughly February–June) and Apertura (played second, roughly August–December). Every 1997 batch added to this platform so far (Boca, Vélez Sarsfield, San Lorenzo) has been Clausura '97 squad data. This page covers Torneo Apertura 1997 instead — the second half of the same year, a separate competition with its own table, top scorer, and ratings. A player can legitimately appear in both with different clubs, different appearance counts, and different ratings; don't treat a name match against the Clausura '97 bio-stub pages as the same tournament performance.
Ratings, not the same schema as the bio-stub pages. El Gráfico's own methodology (see page 26 footnote): a journalist-assigned match rating (1–10 scale, "Promedio" = average across the tournament) for every player who featured, with a 60%-of-matches-played minimum to qualify for these rankings. This is editorial opinion recorded contemporaneously, not a statistical database record — treat "Prom." as a 1997 sportswriter's season-average grade, not an Empire FGA metric.
