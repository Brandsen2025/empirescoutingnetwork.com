🌏 Career Journey — Melbourne to Rotterdam

Transcontinental development: Australian foundation, Belgian refinement, Dutch mastery.

2002-2023 — Melbourne, Australia
Born in Melbourne, Victoria — grew up in Point Cook suburb. Junior football at Hoppers Crossing SC before joining Melbourne City FC academy (2016). Developed through City Football Group system (Guardiola methodology exposure). Professional debut November 2021, scoring first goal April 2022. Won 2x A-League Premierships (2021-22, 2022-23).
2023-2025 — KVC Westerlo, Belgium
Record-breaking transfer — broke A-League transfer record (previously held by Aaron Mooy). Moved to Belgian Pro League for €1.1M (July 2023). Developed defensive robustness and European tactical understanding. 44 appearances, 7 goals — exceptional output for a left-back. Established as premier Australian export.
2025-Present — Feyenoord Rotterdam
Eredivisie ascension. Transferred to Feyenoord for €4.3M (July 2025) — record fee for Australian defender. Immediate impact: Debut assist vs Galatasaray (UCL Qualifier). Named Eredivisie Player of the Month (September 2025) — first Australian ever to win award. Current 2025-26 season: 3 goals, 3 assists in 18 matches. FGA Rating 7.6 confirms top-5 league potential.
2023-Present — Australia National Team
Socceroos regular. Debut March 2023 vs Ecuador. 2023 AFC Asian Cup participant (scored vs India). 23 caps, 2 goals — established as first-choice left-back under Graham Arnold. Featured in 2026 World Cup Qualifiers.
