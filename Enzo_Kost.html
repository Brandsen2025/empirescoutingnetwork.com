Full Scouting Report
Jim Totime · Empire FGA · Apr 2026
Enzo Kost represents the premium modern centre-back prototype emerging from the City Football Group academy at ESTAC Troyes. A 13-year academy product (joined age 6), he has captained every age group and now leads the reserve side in National 3 while training regularly with the Ligue 2 first team.
His left-footedness is a genuine strategic asset — enabling right-sided buildup and diagonal switches. Combined with elite acceleration (9.0/10, GPS 31.88 km/h), he projects as a high-line, inverted centre-back in the Stones/Laporte mold. The 14 career goals from centre-back (including 7 in U19 2024–25) demonstrate exceptional timing and aerial presence in attacking phases.
The DMA composite (8.4/10) reflects elite decision-making in buildup, though one anti-indicator (overestimated pass leading to goal vs Strasbourg B) shows the learning curve of a 19-year-old transitioning to senior football. Character (9.0/10) is elite — three high-pressure pressing recoveries in one quarter demonstrate leadership through action.
The incomplete OBI assessment (OBI_T and OBI_S pending) is the primary blocker. Currently using PROXY_ESTIMATE for SII composite (only SII_A validated). Final rating subject to upward revision to 8.0+ upon completion of pending metrics.

Ligue 2 ready now. Ideal for possession-based systems requiring left-footed buildup initiation. CFG loan network offers optimal development pathway (Lommel SK, Palermo). Market value: €500k–1M rising to €3–5M by 2027. Priority recruitment for progressive clubs.
