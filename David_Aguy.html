🌍 Empire FGA Analysis — The Dual-Nationality Predator

David Aguy represents the new generation of French-Ivorian striking talent emerging from the Parisian suburbs (Banlieue). An 18-year-old centre-forward with dual nationality and elite movement intelligence, Aguy has established himself as one of the top prospects in the RC Strasbourg academy. His FGA rating of 79 reflects high-end youth potential with particular strengths in finishing (82), off-the-ball movement (80), and pace (78). Unlike traditional target men, Aguy operates as a mobile modern striker — capable of stretching defenses, linking play, and finishing clinically inside the box. His 0.39 goals per game in the U19 Championnat National (2024/25) places him in the top 5% of French youth strikers, with scouts noting his natural predatory instincts and calmness in front of goal rare for his age.
