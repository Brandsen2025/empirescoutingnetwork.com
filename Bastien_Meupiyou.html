2006 — Paris Origins
Born March 19, 2006, in Paris to Cameroonian parents. Name "Ndemeni" and "Meupiyou" reflect Cameroonian heritage while "Bastien" and "Chefren" show French/Egyptian influences. Eligible for both France and Cameroon senior teams.
2013-2019 — Parisian Development
Youth career at Paris FC (2013-2018) then FC Montfermeil (2018-2019), developing in the competitive Île-de-France football ecosystem before joining Nantes academy.
2019-2024 — La Jonelière
Joined FC Nantes academy in 2019. Progressed through youth ranks, debuted for Nantes B (18 apps), earned senior debut (Sept 2023 vs Marseille — red card in 9th minute), became France U17/U18 regular.
2024-2025 — Wolves Investment
Signed for Wolverhampton Wanderers (Aug 2024) for £4.2m. Knee injury delayed start. Played 9 games for U21s, reached first-team bench (4 PL squads) but no debut.
July 2025 — Alverca Chapter
Loaned to FC Alverca (Portugal) with buy-back clause. Immediate first-team football in Primeira Liga. Mixed results including the Estoril nightmare, but regular starter (14 starts in 17 apps).
