Philosophy DNA

Core Principle — Football Is 22 Interacting Elements in a Bounded Space

  • Coalition actions — performance is measured by how effectively pairs and trios function together, not by individual output. Lobanovskyi's own term, from the co-authored coaching text with Anatoliy Zelentsov.
  • The three-tier press — full-press (all-out commitment in a zone), half-press (funnel the ball into a channel), false-press (show pressing body shape without commitment, baiting a pass into a trap).
  • The furthest-forward player as first defender — the striker is the pressing trigger. He cuts a passing lane in the 3 seconds after possession is lost; the coalition responds to that individual initiation cue.
  • Interval-based physical profile — sprint recovery cycles, not total distance covered. Lobanovskyi was one of the first to apply structured interval training. Time the pattern, not the total.
  • Drilled combination sequences — the Fan Attack (each pass opens the lane for the next specific action) and the stretched diamond (attacking through three vertical lanes simultaneously). Repeatable, not improvised.
  • Football as a cybernetic system — 22 interacting elements inside a bounded, rule-constrained space. Performance is relational, not individual.

Philosophy Sub-Components 4 + 1 Input

LOBAN_01
Coalition Actions
Weight 25%
Measured by short combination sequences with the same 1–2 teammates repeatedly. Drilled pairings, not random adjacency. The core mechanism of the entire philosophy.
LOBAN_02
Three-Tier Press
Weight 20%
Full-press, half-press, false-press. Log which tier is occurring. A scout who logs "high press" as one undifferentiated event is losing the most important part of the system.
LOBAN_03
Drilled Combinations
Weight 12%
The Fan Attack and stretched diamond. Repeatable, near-identical sequences across different matches. If every attack looks structurally different, the system isn't drilled.
LOBAN_04
Interval Physical Pattern
Weight 8%
Sprint recovery cycles, not total distance. Time the intensity pattern — sprint, controlled recovery jog, sprint again — not the kilometres covered.
INPUT · TIER 2 COMPOSITE
Match Impact Index (MTI)
Weight 35%
MTI = AVERAGE(DYN, SOA). Dynamism and Speed on Action. A Tier 2 composite, not a philosophy sub-component. Captures how much a player changes the match within the coalition structure — the closest composite we have to "coalition efficiency".

Anatoliy Demyanenko9.5 Fit
LB · 1979–1990 · FGA 8.2
Captain of the 1986 and 1988 sides. The definitional Lobanovskyi fullback — defended his flank solo while providing the coalition width. His initiation cue was the trigger for the half-press down the left.
Oleksiy Mykhaylychenko9.5 Fit
CDM · 1984–1991 · FGA 8.2
The coalition anchor. Mykhaylychenko's drilled pairings with Zavarov and Aleinikov were near-identical from match to match. Scored the opening goal in the Euro 1988 final — a set-piece drill, executed as trained.
Aleksandr Zavarov9.5 Fit
RW · 1983–1990 · FGA 8.3
The attacking coalition partner. Zavarov's combination sequences with Mykhaylychenko and Belanov were drilled, not improvised — same shape, same number of touches, same time-to-shot across the 1986-1990 footage.
Serhiy Rebrov9.5 Fit
SS · 1992–2006 · FGA 8.5
Shevchenko's coalition partner. The Rebrov-Shevchenko pairing is the definitive late-Lobanovskyi Fan Attack — two strikers whose movement patterns were drilled to open the lane for each other. 1997-98 was the peak.
