Archetype DNA — Coalition Actions, Three-Tier Press, Soviet Pipeline

Core Principle — Football Is 22 Interacting Elements in a Bounded Space. Expressed by Players from the Soviet and Post-Soviet Pipeline.

  • The archetype rule — every player in this XI expresses the four sub-components of Lobanovskyi Scientific in their own era. No Lobanovskyi-coaching requirement. Scored against their own footage.
  • Zero overlap with the Sacchi XI — the corrected roster shares no starting XI player with the Sacchi Archetype XI. The v1.0 error (6 identical starters) is corrected.
  • Three-tier press — full-press, half-press, false-press. Safonov initiates the false-press from the goalkeeper position. Khusanov presses aggressively from centre-back. Mkhitaryan and Kerzhakov are the definitional full-press operators.
  • Coalition actions — relational efficiency between specific pairs and trios. Tymoshchuk and Rotan are the definitional Ukrainian coalition anchor. Sudakov and Yarmolenko are the Shakhtar-derived drilled pair.
  • Interval physical profile — sprint-recovery-sprint cycles. Tymoshchuk, Rotan, and Zhirkov all express the interval profile.
  • Drilled combinations — near-identical attacking sequences. Rotan, Shomurodov, Mostovoi, and Sudakov participate in recognizable repeating sequences.
Cultural Anchor Principle · New for v1.1 Lobanovskyi built his system inside the Soviet football machine. The players who express his principles most purely come from the same pipeline — the Soviet and post-Soviet academies that drilled the same interval training, the same coalition pairings, and the same three-tier pressing triggers. This XI is built almost entirely from that pipeline. Russian, Ukrainian, Armenian, Uzbek, Kazakh, and Georgian players. Robert Lewandowski is excluded despite his world-class profile because he is Polish and outside the pipeline. The cultural anchor is not cosmetic — it is the framework's answer to the question "who learned this system from the inside?"
Expression-Grade · Not Evidence-Grade · v1.1 Correction Zero Lobanovskyi-coached players. Kaladze, Luzhnyi, and Holovko are excluded — they were Lobanovskyi-coached at Dynamo Kyiv and belong in the Reference XI pool, not the Archetype XI. Karpin, Arshavin, Hleb, and Kvaratskhelia are excluded on sub-component grounds — each has documented failure of the three-tier press. Lewandowski is excluded on cultural anchor grounds.

Anatoliy Tymoshchuk9.80 Fit

He is the on-pitch expression of the Ukrainian pipeline — Shakhtar and Dynamo academies drilling the same principles Lobanovskyi taught at the base level.

The 2022-23 Inter run to the Champions League final, where he played as both a false-nine and a right-sided midfielder within Simone Inzaghi's drilled structure, is the case study. The Armenian pipeline produced him, but the Shakhtar academy made him.
Georgiy Sudakov9.80 Fit

"He has good hold on the ball, making him a press-resistant player." The drilled combinations with Mykola Matviyenko and Yukhym Konoplya are the Shakhtar pattern. He is the current-generation expression of what the Ukrainian pipeline produces.
