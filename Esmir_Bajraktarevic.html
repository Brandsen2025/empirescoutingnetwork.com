Data-Driven Scouting Report — Preliminary
Public sources only · Tier 3 not conducted
Esmir Bajraktarević is a 20-year-old forward/winger born in Appleton, Wisconsin, of Bosnian heritage. He came through the New England Revolution academy, signing a Homegrown deal in May 2022 and making 45 MLS appearances across three seasons (2022-24) before PSV signed him for a record $6M fee on 2 January 2025 — the first Revolution Homegrown Player ever sold overseas, with the club retaining a sell-on percentage.
His first full PSV season produced real output: 4 goals, 4 assists, 1,062 minutes, 6.9 average FotMob rating in the Eredivisie — including a brace against Groningen on matchday one (46', 66'). Note: one source cites a broader all-competition total of 37 games, 7 goals, 4 assists — both figures are presented for transparency rather than silently reconciled into one number.
The international storyline here is exceptional for a player this age. He debuted for the USA in January 2024, then took a one-time switch to Bosnia and Herzegovina, assisted Edin Džeko on the legend's farewell, and — most remarkably — scored the winning penalty in the shootout that eliminated Italy from World Cup qualifying and sent Bosnia to the 2026 World Cup. He made his tournament debut against Canada on 12 June 2026.
Worth being careful about: that World Cup narrative is genuinely major, but it's an international achievement, not a club-level data point. At PSV specifically, 1,062 minutes over a season suggests a rotation or impact role rather than an undisputed starting spot — a distinction worth keeping clear rather than letting the Italy penalty color the club evaluation.
