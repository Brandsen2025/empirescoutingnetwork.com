Yannick Adjoumani represents the modern African full-back archetype — technically gifted, positionally versatile, and tactically adaptable. The 23-year-old Ivorian has established himself as a key starter for Östersunds FK in Sweden's Superettan (2nd tier), playing 2,873 minutes across 32 appearances (89.8 min/game).
