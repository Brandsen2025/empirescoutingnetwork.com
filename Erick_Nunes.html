🌍 Empire FGA Analysis — The Modern Brazilian Defender

Erick Nunes represents the new generation of Brazilian center-backs — combining traditional defensive solidity with modern ball-playing capabilities. At just 21 years old, his FGA rating of 76/100 (7.6/10) places him in the High Potential tier for defenders, showing mature positioning (POS 94) and exceptional composure (CPX 85) rarely seen in players his age. Currently developing at Cercle Brugge in the Belgian Pro League, Nunes demonstrates the calm authority and technical security that suggests a future in Europe's top 5 leagues.

🎭 The Left-Footed Advantage: As a left-footed center-back, Nunes possesses natural balance to play as the left-sided CB in a back three or left center-back in a back four. His 88% pass accuracy includes progressive passes into midfield and diagonal switches to the right flank. In the 2025-26 Belgian Pro League, he's averaging 2.06 tackles and 1.76 interceptions per 90 — elite numbers for a ball-playing defender.
