Club Context

Deportivo Español was founded 12 de octubre de 1956, with Sr. Francisco Ríos Seoane as club president at the time of this guide. Club colors: roja con vivos blancos. Home ground: Santiago de Compostela 3801, Capital. Head coach for the 1986/87 preseason per the guide: Oscar López, Oscar Caballero. Mario Cariaga was one of 22 players listed in the Deportivo Español first-team squad in this edition of the guide, playing as a midfielder (Volante in the source).
