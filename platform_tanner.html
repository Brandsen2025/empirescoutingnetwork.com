FGA RATING SYSTEM — METRIC HIERARCHY EXPLAINED
FGA
FGA Rating Base ★ CORE METRIC
The primary Empire FGA score. Weighted average of all 38 Tier 1+2+3 metrics — your foundation of raw performance stripped of philosophy context. Sort by this to compare players purely on what they deliver on the pitch. Every other metric either feeds into or extends from FGA.
FGA = AVG(21 Tier-1 automated + 6 Tier-2 composite + 11 Tier-3 scout metrics)
Scale: 0–100  ·  25% weight in Ultimate
ULT
Ultimate FGA Rating
FGA elevated by context — philosophy fit and match-level factors layered on top of the base score. Currently blocked platform-wide: several of its required inputs have no defined formula yet, so ULT stays honestly PENDING everywhere until that's resolved.
ULT = FGA + philosophy fit + context (formula incomplete — inputs undefined)
Scale: 0–100  ·  Not yet computable for any player
OBI
Off-Ball Intelligence
Movement quality when not in possession — tactical positioning, spatial awareness, run timing. Scored from three sub-components graded independently on match footage, then combined.
OBI = (OBI_T×0.35) + (OBI_S×0.35) + (OBI_P×0.30)
Scale: 0–10  ·  Tier 3 core metric — requires 3+ events across 3+ matches per sub-component
SII
Sprint Intensity Index
High-intensity movement quality — acceleration, top-speed actions, recovery sprints. Same evidence standard as OBI: three sub-components, each independently validated.
SII = (SII_A×0.40) + (SII_T×0.35) + (SII_R×0.25)
Scale: 0–10  ·  Tier 3 core metric — requires 3+ events across 3+ matches per sub-component
T3
Remaining Video Metrics
The other ten Tier 3 metrics, each graded independently from match footage: Decision Making, Character, Mental Competence, Speed on Action, Spatial Performance, Calmness, Clutch Response, Composure Under Pressure, Dynamism, Creative Risk. Together with OBI and SII, these twelve are what "X of 12 Tier 3 validated" refers to on every player card.
DMA · CHR · MCS · SOA · SPI · CLM · CRS · OMS · DYN · CRK
Scale: 0–10 each  ·  Same validation standard — no shortcuts
PHI
Philosophy-Fit Priority Metrics
Nine metrics required by the 60-manager philosophy formulas (Guardiola, Cruyff, Sacchi, and the rest) — key passes, long/vertical/horizontal passing, dribbling, carrying, tackling, interceptions, shot technique. Added to the grading template but not yet logged for any player. This is why Philosophy Fit shows nothing on any card right now — it isn't broken, it's simply first in line for the next round of video work.
KEY · STK · LGP · VRP · HZP · DRB · CAR · INT · TAC
Scale: 0–10 each  ·  Zero graded so far — real next step, not a formula gap
