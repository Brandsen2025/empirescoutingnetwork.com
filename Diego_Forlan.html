Profile

Diego Forlán Corazo is a retired Uruguayan centre-forward / second striker (1998–2019) who played for Independiente, Manchester United, Villarreal, Atlético Madrid, Inter Milan, Internacional, Cerezo Osaka, Peñarol, Mumbai City and Kitchee. 388 league matches, 159 goals, 44 assists. Two-time Pichichi Trophy and European Golden Shoe winner, and a Europa League / UEFA Super Cup winner with Atlético Madrid. His career peak was the 2010 World Cup, where he won the Golden Ball as the tournament's best player and was joint top scorer with 5 goals, leading Uruguay to the semi-finals under Óscar Tabárez.

The Family Tragedy and the Promise

"Le spese mediche ingenti mandano in crisi finanziaria la famiglia... e Diego promette a papà e mamma che diventerà professionista e pagherà tutte le cure."

— Guerín Sportivo, 2026

At the age of 12, Diego's life was turned upside down when his older sister Alejandra was involved in a terrible car accident that left her confined to a wheelchair. The family's finances were devastated. Diego promised his parents he would become a professional footballer to pay for her medical care. This promise became the defining motivation for his career.

The Menotti Debut — "Divertiti e fai quel sai"

"Mi parlava con naturalezza, come se fossi sempre stato in quella squadra. Mi disse: 'Divertiti e fai quel sai'."

— Diego Forlán on César Luis Menotti, Guerín Sportivo

César Luis Menotti gave Forlán his first-team debut at Independiente on 25 October 1997. The quote is a perfect encapsulation of Menotti's libertad con orden philosophy — giving the player the freedom to express himself on the pitch. It is the strongest system link in this file for the Menotti Liberty philosophy.

The Ferguson Years — "Sapeva Tutto"

"Alex Ferguson era uno che sapeva tutto: preparava i match, ce li spiegava per filo e per segno... Ma nello United realizzai gol importanti, come la doppietta ad Anfield al Liverpool e poi il gol al Chelsea, decisivi per il titolo nazionale 2003."

— Diego Forlán on Alex Ferguson, Guerín Sportivo

Forlán's time at Manchester United was mixed, but he earned a place in the fans' hearts with crucial goals — the double at Anfield in December 2002 and the winner against Chelsea in 2003, both vital for the Premier League title. He acknowledged Ferguson's obsessive match preparation and the leadership of Roy Keane.

The 2010 World Cup

The crowning achievement of his career. Under Óscar Tabárez, Uruguay reached the semi-finals of the 2010 World Cup in South Africa. Forlán was awarded the Golden Ball as the tournament's best player and was joint top scorer with five goals. His stunning volley against Germany in the third-place play-off was voted Goal of the Tournament.
