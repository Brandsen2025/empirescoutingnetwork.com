The Goal — Full Description

57 seconds. Franco Zicarelli flicked a header into the centre circle. A Nueva Chicago player miscontrolled. The ball fell to Monserrat, roughly 42 metres from goal. He took one look up, spotted goalkeeper Facundo Masuero off his line, and — without taking a controlling touch — struck a first-time right-footed half-volley. The ball travelled in a flat, dipping arc over the back-pedaling keeper and into the top corner. 1–0. The goal was the only score of the match, and it ended up being the winner.

The moment was widely shared on Argentine social media, with users immediately calling it a Puskas Award candidate. The clip was carried by TyC Sports, Infobae, Olé, La Nación, and OneFootball. Monserrat's post-match quote: "Muy contento. Siempre que me queda lejos trato de probar y por suerte volvió a entrar." ("Very happy. Whenever the ball falls to me from distance I try my luck, and luckily it went in again.")
