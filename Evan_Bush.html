🌍 Career Journey — The MLS Institutional Memory

From SuperDraft selection to two-time MLS Cup champion and veteran mentor.

2009-2011 — Chicago Fire (Development)
Selected 34th overall in 2009 MLS SuperDraft by Chicago Fire. Spent two seasons as developmental roster player with limited first-team exposure. Loaned to USL-2 teams (Newark Ironbound Express, Vancouver Whitecaps Residency) for playing time.
2011-2018 — Montreal Impact (Prime Years)
Established as MLS starter. Signed with Montreal Impact (2011), becoming primary goalkeeper through their MLS expansion years. 152 appearances, 42 clean sheets. Peak athletic period (ages 25-32) with career-high save percentage (72.4% in 2016). Played in 2015 CONCACAF Champions League Final (defeat to Club América).
2019-2021 — Columbus Crew (First Spell)
Championship pedigree. Joined Columbus Crew as experienced backup. Played critical role in 2020 MLS Cup victory (COVID-affected season), starting 3 matches during championship run. Added 2021 Campeones Cup title. Professional locker room presence during Caleb Porter's tenure.
2022 — Vancouver Whitecaps (Brief)
Short-term stopgap. Traded to Vancouver as temporary solution during goalkeeper injury crisis. 9 appearances before returning to Columbus following season.
2023-Present — Columbus Crew (Veteran Phase)
Mentorship and emergency duty. Third-choice goalkeeper behind Patrick Schulte and Nicholas Hagen during 2023 MLS Cup-winning campaign (limited minutes but credited leadership role). Current role: Cup competitions and injury cover. 6 appearances in 2025-26 season showing functional competence.
The Institutional Knowledge Factor: Bush's 16-season MLS tenure spans three clubs and multiple tactical eras (from Preki's defensive Montreal to Caleb Porter's possession Columbus to Nancy's modern build-up). This tactical multilingualism makes him valuable as a training ground coach and matchday tactical advisor to younger goalkeepers. His 2020 MLS Cup ring provides championship credibility in the locker room despite reduced playing time.
