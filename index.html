The System

Most scouting reports
show you what happened.
Ours shows you why.

Every Empire FGA metric ties back to a real, timestamped moment on the pitch — a run, a pass, a positional read — graded against a 60-Philosophy framework built from the tactical DNA of Cruyff, Guardiola, Bielsa, Ferguson, Sacchi, and 56 other football minds. No black-box AI score. No stat you can't trace back to footage.

1
779 Metrics, Not ~200
Traditional providers like Opta and StatsBomb track roughly 200 data points per player. Empire FGA tracks 779 — spanning attacking, passing, defensive, possession, goalkeeping, and 240 philosophy-fit sub-metrics.
2
60-Philosophy Tactical DNA
Every player is scored 0–10 against 60 named football philosophies across 9 schools (Dutch, Spanish, Italian, English, German, French, South American, Eastern European, Modern Masters) — so you know not just how good a player is, but whether they actually fit your system.
3
Hybrid AI + Human Intelligence
Automated data collection handles the volume. Trained scouts handle the judgment calls a spreadsheet can't make — decision-making, composure, character. Every number is either machine-verified or human-graded from footage, and we tell you which.
4
Cross-Era Comparison (ERA)
Our Era-Relative Adjustment lets you compare a current prospect against how a player performed in a different footballing era — the same way you'd compare a 2026 midfielder to a 1988 or 1999 reference point, on a level scale.
