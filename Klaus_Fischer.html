Profile

Klaus Fischer — nicknamed Il re della rovesciata ("The King of the Bicycle Kick") — is a retired West German centre-forward who played for 1860 Munich, Schalke 04, 1. FC Köln and VfL Bochum between 1968 and 1988. 535 Bundesliga matches, 268 goals, 45 caps and 32 goals for West Germany, a 1982 World Cup finalist. His defining trait was acrobatic, physically resilient finishing — he scored three German Goals of the Year via bicycle kick, including the 1977 "Goal of the Century" against Switzerland.
