Profile

Néstor Ariel Fabbri is a 29-year-old Argentine central defender born on 29 April 1968 in Capital Federal, Argentina. 181cm, 78kg. This assessment covers his Clausura 1997 season at Boca Juniors, where he was an undisputed starter, making 13 appearances and scoring 2 goals. He had a long career before Boca, with 177 appearances for Racing Club (where he won the Supercopa under Alfio Basile), a brief stint at América de Cali, and 46 appearances for Lanús. A senior international for Argentina, he was part of the 1990 World Cup squad under Carlos Bilardo (playing the opener against Cameroon) and was regularly called up by Passarella for World Cup qualifying and the 1995 Copa América.
