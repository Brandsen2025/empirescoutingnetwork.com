Data-Driven Scouting Report — Preliminary
Public sources only · Tier 3 not conducted
Joey Veerman is a 27-year-old Dutch central midfielder for PSV Eindhoven, born 19 November 1998 in Volendam. 185cm, right-footed, wears No. 23. Came through FC Volendam's academy, made his senior debut at 17 (9 Sep 2016), joined SC Heerenveen in 2019 (71 Eredivisie apps, 14 goals, 21 assists over three seasons), then signed for PSV on 5 January 2022 for a fee reported around €6M. Contract to 30 June 2028 per Transfermarkt. This report is built entirely from public statistical sources (FotMob, Wikipedia) and press coverage of his Netherlands standing and transfer situation — it is not a Tier 3 video-scouted assessment.
2025-26 — his best PSV season by creative output. 14 assists (league-high in the Eredivisie), 27 big chances created (also league-high), 8 goals, 2,370 minutes across 30 appearances, 7.96 average FotMob rating. 100th percentile in both chance creation and progressive passing per available data — the strongest statistical signal on this entire card. Shot numbers are modest by comparison (33 shots, 42.42% on target, npxG/90 of 0.28), consistent with a playmaker who creates far more than he finishes.
Availability had real gaps. He was unavailable for PSV's 23 August 2025 win over Groningen, and missed the 11 April 2026 Sparta Rotterdam match with an injury (Noah Fernández started in his place). Neither absence is explained in the sources found beyond "unavailable" / "injury" — worth confirming with Jim's own footage review if the pattern recurs.
The Netherlands situation is the defining storyline right now. Debuted for the Oranje 18 June 2023 (sub for Mats Wieffer vs Italy). Led the Eredivisie with 130 chances created in 2023-24, earning a Euro 2024 call-up — then was hooked after 33-35 minutes of the group finale against Austria, with Koeman publicly attributing it to decision-making under pressure ("tripping over the ball, giving the ball away") rather than form or fitness. He has not been capped since: 16 caps, 1 goal total. He was left off both Koeman's 55-man preliminary list and the final World Cup 2026 squad, and pushed back publicly in late 2025, saying the exclusion was framed around "character" rather than performance and that he was "done with it."
Transfer speculation has not become fact. A reported €23M Fenerbahçe release clause activated 1 January 2026; Veerman himself declined to sign, and PSV confirmed he'd stay at least through summer 2026. Brentford links surfaced at various points without a move materializing. He remains at PSV as of this report, contract to 2028.
