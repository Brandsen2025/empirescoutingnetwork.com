🌍 Scout Assessment Overview

Lautaro Martínez, "El Toro," is currently operating at the peak of his powers as Inter Milan's captain. He defines the modern "Complete Forward" archetype—combining the low center of gravity and link-up play of a number 10 with the penalty-box predation of a number 9. His defensive work rate (AGG 10.0) sets the tempo for Inter's entire pressing system. Despite his height (1.74m), his aerial timing is elite, often outjumping taller defenders. He has evolved into a clutch leader, consistently scoring in high-stakes matches (Derby della Madonnina, UCL knockouts).

Key Weapon: The "Turning Strike." Lautaro excels at receiving the ball with his back to goal, using his immense lower-body strength to hold off defenders before spinning aggressively to fire powerful shots across the keeper.
