🌍 Empire FGA Analysis — The Dakar Destroyer

Pape Diong represents the next generation of Senegalese midfield enforcers — a physically imposing defensive midfielder standing at 1.93m, combining raw athletic power with surprising technical composure for his age. Hailed as one of Africa's most exciting midfield talents, Diong made the leap from Dakar's AF Darou Salam academy to French professional football with RC Strasbourg, and is now gaining crucial senior experience on loan at USL Dunkerque in Ligue 2.
