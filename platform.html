FGA RATING SYSTEM — METRIC HIERARCHY EXPLAINED
FGA
FGA Base (Tier 1+2) ★ CORE METRIC
Stats-anchored base score built from Tier 1 and Tier 2 metrics. Tier 3 scout grading is included only for players who have completed it (most have not), so this is not a full FGA rating on its own — your foundation of raw performance stripped of philosophy context. Sort by this to compare players purely on what they deliver on the pitch. Every other metric either feeds into or extends from FGA.
FGA = AVG(21 Tier-1 automated + 6 Tier-2 composite + 11 Tier-3 scout metrics)
Scale: 0–100  ·  25% weight in Ultimate
ULT
Ultimate FGA Rating
FGA elevated by tactical DNA, creative control, clutch factor and harmonic style. The original spec self-referenced (ULT required its own MZ column) — fixed by folding MZ's 0.20 weight into HAR. Known unresolved issue: FGA/THI run on a 0–100 scale, CCI/CLU/HAR run on 0–10 — averaging them directly without rescaling is mathematically broken. Not corrected yet, flagged here rather than silently patched. Blocked platform-wide: THI has no formula, CTX is undefined. CCI/CLU/HAR require Tier-3 grading — as of 2026-08-31, 7 current players are fully graded (Beukema, Etou, Masereka, Luna, Gabriel Mec, Gemelson, Federico Dimarco) but the scale mismatch above means their ULT still can't be computed correctly; the 0.20/0.15/0.10/0.30 weights would mix 0–100 and 0–10 inputs. Two additional records — Ronaldo (1996–97) and Ronaldinho Gaucho (1998) — also carry full Tier-3 grading but are historical benchmark entries, not current prospects, and are excluded from this count. Not "nobody graded" anymore — just still blocked by the unfixed formula.
ULT = (FGA×0.25) + (THI×0.15) + (CCI×0.15) + (CLU×0.10) + (HAR×0.30) + (CTX×0.05)
Scale mismatch unresolved (0–100 vs 0–10 inputs)  ·  Not yet computable for any player
THI
Tactical Harmony Index
How fluidly a player integrates across tactical systems — positional intelligence (POI), off-ball coordination (OBI), system adaptability (COACH). Still qualitative only: the source describes three pillars but defines no weights or arithmetic between them. Nobody has ever received a THI score.
No closed-form formula exists yet — three qualitative pillars, unweighted
Scale: 0–100 (per source)  ·  15% weight in ULT  ·  Not implemented
CCI
Creative Control Index
Ability to impose will through inventive decisions and unpredictability under pressure — separates technically correct players from game-changers. Liga 1 Perú has no FBref shot-creation table, so SCA90 there falls back to a Sofascore bigChancesCreated-per-90 proxy.
CCI = (CRK×0.4) + (DMA×0.3) + (SOA×0.2) + (SCA90_Normalized×0.1)
Scale: 0–10  ·  15% weight in ULT  ·  0 players graded — needs Tier-3 CRK/DMA/SOA
CLU
Clutch Factor
Performance gap between a player's average output and their output in decisive moments — injury time, must-win knockouts. Unresolved scale issue in the source formula: LOG_Pressure_Factor runs 0–6 (per the Level-of-Game framework) while CRS/CLM/MCS run 0–10, and it's blended in at 10% weight with no rescaling. Flagged, not fixed.
CLU = (CRS×0.5) + (CLM×0.2) + (MCS×0.2) + (LOG_Pressure_Factor×0.1)
Scale: 0–10 nominal (LOG term unrescaled)  ·  10% weight in ULT  ·  0 players graded
HAR
Harmonic Style
The artistic dimension — spatial poetry, flow, individual flair that box scores miss but video scouts prioritize. The original spec self-referenced (HAR required its own LZ column) and double-counted CRK; fixed by merging both CRK terms into one CRK×0.5 term.
HAR = (CRK×0.5) + (SPI×0.3) + (Dribble_Success_Normalized×0.2)
Scale: 0–10  ·  30% weight in ULT  ·  0 players graded — needs Tier-3 CRK/SPI
OBI
Off-Ball Intelligence
Movement quality when not in possession — tactical positioning, spatial awareness, run timing. Scored from three sub-components graded independently on match footage, then combined.
OBI = (OBI_T×0.35) + (OBI_S×0.35) + (OBI_P×0.30)
Scale: 0–10  ·  Tier 3 core metric — requires 3+ events across 3+ matches per sub-component
SII
Sprint Intensity Index
High-intensity movement quality — acceleration, top-speed actions, recovery sprints. Same evidence standard as OBI: three sub-components, each independently validated.
SII = (SII_A×0.40) + (SII_T×0.35) + (SII_R×0.25)
Scale: 0–10  ·  Tier 3 core metric — requires 3+ events across 3+ matches per sub-component
T3
Remaining Video Metrics
The other ten Tier 3 metrics, each graded independently from match footage: Decision Making, Character, Mental Competence, Speed on Action, Spatial Performance, Calmness, Clutch Response, Composure Under Pressure, Dynamism, Creative Risk. Together with OBI and SII, these twelve are what "X of 12 Tier 3 validated" refers to on every player card.
DMA · CHR · MCS · SOA · SPI · CLM · CRS · OMS · DYN · CRK
Scale: 0–10 each  ·  Same validation standard — no shortcuts
PHI
Philosophy-Fit Priority Metrics
Nine metrics required by the 80-manager philosophy formulas (Guardiola, Cruyff, Sacchi, and the rest) — key passes, long/vertical/horizontal passing, dribbling, carrying, tackling, interceptions, shot technique. First graded for Sam Beukema (2026-08-18, full 9-metric video pass, ≥3-event confidence). Still not wired into a live Trophy Magnet Factor or Philosophy Fit computation anywhere in this platform — the data exists, the formula that consumes it doesn't yet.
KEY · STK · LGP · VRP · HZP · DRB · CAR · INT · TAC
Scale: 0–10 each  ·  1 of 8,495 players graded — Beukema
