Profile

A right-footed central defender, per the source, strong defensively but rarely bringing the ball forward himself. A genuine threat in the air on set pieces, which has produced several goals in Argentine football.

Career to date (as of mid-1997)

Arrived in Argentina in 1994 from Olimpia of Paraguay, joining Rosario Central and debuting in Primera División on 11 September that year, playing as líbero. River signed him for Clausura '95 to replace Roberto Ayala, who had just been sold to Italy — took time to win the fans over but became a fixture. A regular for the Paraguay national team as cover for Catalino Rivarola and Carlos Gamarra, capped at the 1993, 1995 and 1997 Copa América tournaments and a key part of Paraguay's 1998 World Cup qualification campaign.
