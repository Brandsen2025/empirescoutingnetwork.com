🌍 Empire FGA Analysis — The Moroccan Shield

Oussama EL Azzouzi represents the archetype of the modern defensive midfielder forged in the Dutch Eredivisie, refined in the Bundesliga, and now proving his worth in Ligue 1. His career trajectory demonstrates steady upward progression from Utrecht's academy to Union Berlin's European campaigns, now seeking consistency in France's top flight.
