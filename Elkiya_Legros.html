🌍 Empire FGA Analysis — The Franco-Congolese Prospect

Elikya Legros represents one of the most promising defensive prospects in French football, combining the technical education of the Auxerre academy with the physical attributes and heritage of DR Congo. At just 17 years old, the centre-back has already established himself as a "key pillar" in Auxerre's U19 defense, earning his first professional contract in June 2025 — a significant milestone for a player of his age. His elegant playing style, intelligent tackling, and impressive maturity have marked him as a generational talent for the Burgundy club.
