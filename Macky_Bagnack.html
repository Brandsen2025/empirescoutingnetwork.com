🌍 Career Journey — From La Masia to the Balkans

Fourteen-year odyssey across seven countries and nine clubs.

2008-2012 — Samuel Eto'o Academy → La Masia
Discovered by Samuel Eto'o Foundation in Cameroon at age 13. Moved to Barcelona's legendary La Masia academy. Developed technical excellence and tactical understanding under Barcelona methodology. Graduated to Barcelona B in 2012.
2012-2016 — Barcelona B (Segunda División)
43 appearances, 1 goal. Made professional debut age 17. Established as regular starter in 2013-14 (24 apps). Suffered relegation in 2014-15 with 18 apps, 11 yellows, 1 red — disciplinary issues emerged. Contract rescinded Jan 2016.
2016-2017 — Real Zaragoza
First senior move. 8 appearances in Segunda División. Limited playing time hindered development. Learned Spanish professional game but failed to establish as starter. Released after one season.
2017-2018 — Admira Wacker (Austria)
Disastrous move. Only 1 appearance in Austrian Bundesliga. Complete loss of form and confidence. Career at crossroads — dropped from promising youth to peripheral squad player.
2018-2020 — NK Olimpija Ljubljana
Career resurrection. 62 appearances, 2 goals. Won Slovenian Cup 2018/19. Established as reliable centre-back in PrvaLiga. Developed aerial dominance (65%+ duels). Form attracted Serbian SuperLiga interest.
2020-2021 — Partizan Belgrade
Big move to Serbian giants. 18 appearances, 4 Europa League qualifiers. Played alongside high-quality teammates. Injuries limited impact (hamstring, 6 weeks). Learned organizational defending in high-pressure environment.
2021-2023 — FC Kairat Almaty
Financially lucrative move to Kazakhstan. 45 appearances, 2 goals. Won Kazakhstan Cup 2021. Played in Europa Conference League qualifiers. Loaned to Russian Premier League (Pari NN) Jan 2023 — only 1 appearance, injury curtailed spell.
2024-2025 — TSC Bačka Topola
Return to Serbia. 6 appearances, 1 goal. Served as experienced backup for Serbian SuperLiga side. Contract expired June 2025, became free agent.
Jan 2026 — ND Primorje
Signed for Slovenian 2. SNL side ND Primorje. Brings wealth of experience to promotion-chasing squad. Reunited with Slovenian football environment where he previously succeeded. Current Role
The Journeyman's Survival: Bagnack's career exemplifies adaptation over stardom. While La Masia peers like Sergi Roberto or Rafinha reached Barcelona first team, Bagnack forged a different path — international journeyman. His ability to reinvent himself after the Admira disaster (2017-18) shows resilience. The Olimpija success (2018-20) proved he could thrive in the right environment. His return to Slovenia with Primorje suggests a desire to end career on positive note in familiar territory where his strengths (aerial ability, technical comfort) are valued.
