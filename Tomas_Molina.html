🌍 Career Journey — The Hard Road to Primera

Peripatetic development: Lower league grinding through Buenos Aires football ecosystem.

1995 — Born: Buenos Aires, Argentina
Born in Buenos Aires Province. Early football education in Huracán youth academy, absorbing Argentine football culture from age 10. Developed through the combative Buenos Aires football circuit.
2016-2019 — Huracán & Loan Spells
Professional debut with Huracán (2016). Limited opportunities (4 appearances) led to loan to Almirante Brown (2018) in Primera B Metropolitana. Lower league education: 15 apps, 0 goals but crucial physical development.
2019-2021 — National Ascent
Goal-scoring breakthrough. Brown de Adrogue (2019-20): 5 goals in 21 matches. Ferro Carril Oeste (2020-21): 10 goals in 27 matches — established as Primera Nacional goal threat. Developed target forward skillset.
2022 — Ecuador Breakthrough (LDU Quito)
International breakthrough. Transferred to LDU Quito (Ecuador Serie A). 13 goals in 27 appearances — elite goal-scoring rate. Won Copa Sudamericana 2022 (2 goals in 8 continental matches). First major trophy.
2023-2024 — Mexican & Argentine Rotation
Mixed success period. FC Juárez (Liga MX): Adaptation struggles (4 goals in 13 matches). Returned to Argentina via Talleres Córdoba and loan to Central Córdoba (2024). 5 goals in 17 matches — solid if unspectacular.
2024-Present — Argentinos Juniors Renaissance
Career peak. Joined Argentinos Juniors (July 2024). 2025 season: 10 goals in 31 matches — established as Primera División goal threat. Contract until December 2029 indicates long-term club commitment. FGA Rating 6.7 confirms top-tier Argentine forward status.
