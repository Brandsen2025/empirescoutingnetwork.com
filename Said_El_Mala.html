🌍 Player Background

Said El Mala is a German winger who broke through as one of the Bundesliga's standout young attackers in 2025-26. Born in Krefeld to a German mother and a Lebanese father (a former centre-back at Linner SV), he came through the youth ranks at Linner SV, KFC Uerdingen 05, Borussia Mönchengladbach and TSV Meerbusch before joining Viktoria Köln's academy in 2023. He signed his first professional contract with Viktoria Köln in February 2024 and made his senior debut that month in 3. Liga. His form there prompted 1. FC Köln to sign him in June 2024, though he was immediately loaned back to Viktoria Köln for the 2024-25 season, where he scored 13 goals in 32 appearances and was named the 2024-25 3. Liga Newcomer of the Season. He then established himself in FC Köln's first team for the 2025-26 Bundesliga season, scoring 13 goals in 34 appearances, and has continued into 2026-27 wearing squad number 10 on a contract extended through 2031. His brother, Malek El Mala, is also a professional footballer.
