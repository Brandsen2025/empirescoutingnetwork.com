David operates as a classic target man in Union SG's 3-4-3 or 4-2-3-1 systems. He drops deep to link play but his primary weapon is the penalty box presence. Defensively, he contributes 2.48 clearances per 90 — showing willingness to defend set-pieces and help his backline. Set-piece threat: His aerial ability makes him dangerous from corners and crosses. 🌍 Career Journey — From College Soccer to Pro League Stardom

Five-year journey from Canadian university football to Belgian Pro League goal machine.

2019-2021 — Niagara University
Dominant college career with Niagara Purple Eagles (NCAA Division I). Scored 15 goals in 32 appearances, showcasing raw physical ability and instinctive finishing. Developed technical foundation in the Canadian university system while studying — emphasizing intelligent movement and physical conditioning. All-Conference selection in sophomore year.
2021-2022 — FC Edmonton
First professional steps. Signed with FC Edmonton in the Canadian Premier League. 8 goals in 24 appearances — adapting to men's football while maintaining academic commitments. Learned to use his frame against physical defenders. The CPL's competitive level (similar to USL Championship) provided perfect bridge to European football.
2022-2023 — KV Kortrijk
Belgian breakthrough. Signed by KV Kortrijk after impressive CPL season. 6 goals in 28 appearances (mostly substitute) — adapting to Jupiler Pro League intensity. Worked under coach Bernd Storck — learning tactical discipline and defensive positioning. Physical development accelerated — added muscle mass without losing pace.
2023-Present — Union Saint-Gilloise
Elite Belgian football. Transferred to Union SG — Champions League qualification contenders. Immediate impact: 9 goals in 23 games (2024-25), often from the bench. His goals-per-minute ratio is league-leading. Developed partnership with attacking midfielders — Teddy Teuma and Lazare Amani supply the through balls he thrives on. Career trajectory: From college soccer to Europa League football in 5 years — remarkable rise.
The Late Bloomer Advantage: David's unconventional path (college → CPL → Belgium) means he has lower mileage than 24-year-old academy products. His body is fresh, his hunger is intact, and his character (9.8/10) reflects gratitude for the opportunity. Unlike young prospects who expect success, David earns it every game. Scouting insight: Players with non-traditional paths often have higher character scores and lower injury rates — David exemplifies this.
