📐 Methodology — LOG-Adjusted FGA

Every league in the Empire FGA system carries a LOG (Level of Game) tier. This tier reflects the quality of the competition — from World Cup Final (5.0) down to amateur level (1.5).

Adjusted FGA = Player FGA × (Target League LOG ÷ 3.5)
3.5 = neutral baseline (matches the master LOG_Adjusted_FGA formula in the LOG Implementation Manual)

Base FGA used: 8.4 — Francescoli's rating in Argentine Primera (LOG 3.4).

Reading the number: A result above the base FGA means the target league is a tougher test than the source — the rating is scaled up accordingly. A result below the base means an easier league. 3.5 is neutral: a LOG-3.5 league returns the base FGA unchanged.

This projection assumes the player would produce at a similar level, adjusted only for competition strength. Actual performance varies by tactical fit, teammates, era, and role.
