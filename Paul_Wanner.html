Paul Wanner is a 20-year-old attacking midfielder for PSV Eindhoven, born 23 December 2005 in Dornbirn, Austria, to German parents (dual citizenship from birth). 185cm, left-footed, wears No. 10. Joined Bayern Munich's academy at 12, became the club's youngest-ever Bundesliga debutant (16y15d, 7 Jan 2022) and youngest-ever Champions League player (16y293d, 12 Oct 2022). This report is built entirely from public statistical sources (FotMob, Wikipedia) and press coverage of his transfer and international-allegiance switch — it is not a Tier 3 video-scouted assessment.
Loan development — productive, if modest, at both stops. SV Elversberg (2023-24, 2. Bundesliga): 6 goals, 4 assists in 28 appearances. 1. FC Heidenheim (2024-25): 41 appearances across all competitions (6 goals, 4 assists), including 29 Bundesliga appearances (3 goals, 2 assists). Bundesliga.com's own tactical coverage described him as Heidenheim's classic No. 10.
2025-26 at PSV — an adaptation season on the numbers. 3 goals, 3 assists, 1,641 minutes across 22 Eredivisie appearances, 7.46 average FotMob rating. At roughly 75 minutes per appearance, this reads as a rotation role rather than a first-choice one — plausible given PSV's squad depth and Wanner adjusting to a new league in the same year he also switched international allegiance. 5 yellow cards is a comparatively high rate for the minutes played, worth tracking rather than dismissing.

The international story is the headline of this card. 21 caps and 7 goals for Germany at various youth levels, including a goal on his U21 debut (Oct 2024 vs Bulgaria). In January 2026, reports surfaced that he was considering Austria — the country of his birth — largely because Austria had already secured World Cup qualification while Germany's midfield picture was crowded.
