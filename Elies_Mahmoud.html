Full Scouting Report
Jim Totime · Empire FGA · Apr 2026
Elies Mahmoud is a technical, left‑footed right winger who excels in possession‑based systems. His dribbling success (60.4%) is elite for the Superliga, ranking in the top 15% for wingers. He consistently cuts inside to shoot or combine. However, his xA per 90 (0.22) is below average, indicating that his final ball lacks consistent quality. Video analysis confirms flashes of creativity (CRK 8.0) but also inconsistent speed of action (SOA 6.6) and occasional poor decisions under pressure.
Physical limitations are clear: aerial duel success (42.9%) and defensive contributions are below average. The video log highlights slow decision‑making at times and a reluctance to use his right foot, making him predictable.
Empire FGA Final Verdict (Provisional)
Specialist Creator — Value Play. Mahmoud is a ready‑made creative wide player for mid‑table Superliga or Ligue 2 sides. His ceiling is limited by physicality, but his technical floor is solid.
