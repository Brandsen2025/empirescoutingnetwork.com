What We Sell
A player intelligence platform that tells you not just what a player did — but who they are tactically.
Competitors like Opta and StatsBomb track ~200 stats. Empire FGA tracks 779 metrics, layers them against 60 coaching philosophies, and adjusts for league difficulty (LOG system). The output: objective fit scores between a player and any tactical system.
How the Data Works
Tier 1
Automated (60%) — pulled from FBref. Goals, passes, defensive actions. Fully objective.
Tier 2
Composite (30%) — formulas combining Tier 1 data. Composure, mobility, creative index.
Tier 3
Scout-observed (10%) — video analysis only. Decision-making, character, clutch response. This is where we separate from pure stat platforms.
Never present Tier 2 scores as if they're scouted. If video hasn't been done, the card shows VIDEO_PENDING. Do not invent scores.

Objection Handling
"We already use Wyscout / Opta."
Those platforms track events. We track tactical DNA. They tell you what happened. We tell you whether a player fits your coach's system. They're complementary, not competing.
"Do you have validated results?"
We have active pilots running and a historical validation study (Fernando Redondo cohort, Argentina 1985). We don't claim 85% accuracy figures we can't prove. What we can show is the methodology — which is the strongest case we have.
"Who else is using this?"
We're in early commercial stage. Two active pilots in progress. This is an honest position — and the right buyers respect it more than fake testimonials. Lead with the demo, not social proof.
"It's too expensive."
Offer the €500 / 5-player pilot. 10-day delivery. The goal is one signed transfer decision they can attribute to the data. That's the conversion moment.
