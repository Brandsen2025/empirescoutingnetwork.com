🌍 Career Journey — From Rotterdam to Lisbon

Five-country journey from Dutch youth football to Benfica's first team.

2002-2021 — Rotterdam Roots
Born in Rotterdam to Cape Verdean parents. Developed at Feyenoord and FC Twente academies before moving to Sweden (Helsingborg U21). Dual-nationality eligibility (Netherlands/Cape Verde) but committed to Cape Verde senior team in 2023.
2022-2025 — German Apprenticeship
Rot-Weiß Erfurt (2022-23): 52 games, 3 goals in Regionalliga. Viktoria Köln (2023-25): 50 games, 3 goals, 8 assists in 3. Liga. Developed defensive discipline and crossing consistency in physical German lower leagues. Earned first Cape Verde cap in 2023.
2025-2026 — Estrela Breakthrough
The revelation. Signed for €300K from Viktoria Köln. Scored 5 goals in 15 games from right-back, including a hat-trick at Casa Pia. 114 crosses, 11 interceptions, 10 tackles won. His offensive output from defense made him Primeira Liga's most productive fullback in first half of season.
Dec 2025 — Benfica Signing
The big move. Benfica paid €6M (90% rights), potentially rising to €8.5M with objectives. Signed contract until 2030 with €60M release clause. Made immediate impact with assist on debut vs Estoril. Mourinho identified him as "aggressiveness, strength, and speed" solution for squad depth.
The Jovane Connection: Cabral's signing was facilitated by intelligence from Jovane Cabral (his teammate at Estrela and Cape Verde international), who vouched for his character to Benfica scouts. This network effect — Cape Verdean diaspora connections linking Dutch, German, and Portuguese football — illustrates the globalization of modern scouting.
