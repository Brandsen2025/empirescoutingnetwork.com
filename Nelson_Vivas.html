Profile

A right-sided player capable at both full-back and wide midfield, and occasionally used as a central defender, though the source rates that his weakest role. Covers a lot of ground, gets forward with purpose down the right and delivers accurate crosses; also has a powerful shot he used more often before Boca than since arriving.

Career to date (as of mid-1997)

Spotted by Daniel Passarella while at Quilmes, where he was regarded as one of the best emerging right-backs in the country, though the national team call-up only came once he'd moved to Boca. Started out at Somisa, a small club in his hometown; his father brought him to a Quilmes trial that led straight to a contract. Was voted the best player of Quilmes' 1990/91 Nacional B promotion-winning season, then made his Primera División debut on 10 September 1991 against Newell's Old Boys, playing 35 matches and scoring once as Quilmes were relegated straight back down. Moved to Boca for Apertura '94. Capped once for Argentina, starting in a 1-1 draw with South Africa, but not recalled after a below-par showing.
