Profile

A 26-year-old Australian central midfielder born in Port Macquarie, New South Wales. Developed through the Newcastle Jets youth academy before making his professional debut in January 2018 at age 17. Spent 6 seasons at Newcastle Jets (116 apps, 11 goals), winning the A-League Men Young Footballer of the Year award in 2021-22. Moved to Western United in May 2023 on a three-year deal, making 55 appearances and winning the club's Player of the Season award in 2024-25. Following Western United's conditional hibernation, joined Western Sydney Wanderers in September 2025 on a three-year contract. Described as an extremely versatile midfielder who has played in defensive, central, and attacking midfield roles, as well as wide areas and up front. Holds the record as the youngest player in A-League Men history to reach 150 appearances (December 2024, age 24). A-League All Star 2024 and PFA Team of the Season 2024-25. Represented Australia U19 and Australia U23 (Olyroos).
