Career Timeline
Born 29 Oct 2002 · Melbourne, Australia 🇦🇺
Dutch-Australian heritage. Father from Wierden, Netherlands (near Almelo); grew up in Point Cook, Melbourne. Played junior football for Hoppers Crossing SC. Brother Kasey Bos also professional (Excelsior Rotterdam).
2016 — 2020 · Melbourne City FC Academy
A-League academy. Joined Melbourne City academy at 14, developed through youth ranks. Signed first professional contract in September 2021 (three-year deal).
2021 — 2023 · Melbourne City (Senior)
A-League breakthrough. Debut 27 Nov 2021 vs Adelaide United. First goal 6 Apr 2022 vs Sydney FC (4-0). 41 A-League apps, 3 goals, 6 assists. Won A-League Premiership 2021-22 and 2022-23. PFA Harry Kewell Medal 2023.
May 2023 — Record Transfer to KVC Westerlo
Record A-League outbound transfer. Joined Belgian Pro League side Westerlo for undisclosed fee, breaking Melbourne City and A-League transfer records. Four-year contract. 44 apps, 7 goals across two seasons. Hamstring surgery limited minutes in final season (18 apps).
28 Mar 2023 — Socceroos Debut
International debut. Came on as substitute vs Ecuador at Docklands Stadium. First of 31 caps (and counting). Scored first goal vs India at 2023 AFC Asian Cup (13 Jan 2024).
25 Jul 2025 — Feyenoord Transfer
Dutch giants. Moved to Feyenoord for reported €5 million on four-year contract until 2029. Father's family from region; Feyenoord was father's club. Immediate integration into first team.
Aug 2025 — Champions League Debut
Elite European debut. Started vs Fenerbahçe in UCL qualifier. Assisted 91st-minute winner (Anis Hadj Moussa) on debut. Announced himself on continental stage immediately.
Sep 2025 — Eredivisie Player of the Month
Historic achievement. Named Eredivisie POTM for September 2025 — first Australian ever to win the award. Scored from outside the box vs AZ Alkmaar (3-3). 4 goal involvements in first 6 games.
31 May 2026 — 2026 FIFA World Cup Selection
World Cup. Selected in Australia's 26-man squad for 2026 FIFA World Cup in North America. Recorded top speed of 36.7km/h in tournament preparation.
