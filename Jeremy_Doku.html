Empire FGA · Apr 2026 · T3 PENDING
Jeremy Doku is a top-tier Premier League winger defined by elite physical acceleration and exceptional off-ball movement. His Sprint Intensity Index (SII 9.52) places him in the top 3 among EPL wide players, combining explosive burst with genuine top-end velocity. At Manchester City, he operates as an inverted left winger cutting onto his stronger right foot — a profile that maximises directness against set defensive structures.
His Ghost Run Index (GRI 9.0) stands as the signature metric. He routinely identifies and runs into dangerous channels ahead of the ball — an anticipatory off-ball pattern consistent with Cruyff's forward movement principles. His progressive carry rate (4.2 dribbles/90, EPL #1) confirms the on-ball translation of that spatial intelligence.
The primary analytical question that T3 will resolve is decision-making quality at the end of actions. His carrier stats are elite; his G+A volume is below the expected output of a player generating that volume of entries into the final third. Possible explanations include: system-level constraint (Man City's rotational use), finishing ceiling (SOA pending), or composure under last-action pressure (CLM pending).
T3 Observation Priority: DMA (final-third decisions), CLM (composure on last action), CRS (clutch output in decisive moments), and OMS (transition speed — does the GRI translate to physical transition or is it anticipation without execution?) are the four metrics that will determine whether this profile is a €70M+ asset or a €45M carrier volume statistic.
