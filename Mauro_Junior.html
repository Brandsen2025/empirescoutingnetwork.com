Mauro Jaqueson Júnior Ferreira dos Santos, known as Mauro Júnior, is a 27-year-old Brazilian left-back for PSV Eindhoven, born 6 May 1999 in Palmital, São Paulo. 171cm. He's now in his ninth season at PSV, having signed a five-year deal in June 2017 out of Desportivo Brasil's youth setup and taken only one loan spell — a full 2019-20 season at Heracles Almelo (26 apps, 6G, 6A). This report is built entirely from public statistical sources (FotMob, Wikipedia, Transfermarkt) and press coverage — it is not a Tier 3 video-scouted assessment.
The 2025-26 numbers are genuinely strong for the position. 1 goal, 9 assists, 2,086 minutes, 7.72 average FotMob rating, only 2 yellow cards. FotMob's own positional comparison notes he rates highly on both rating and assists relative to Eredivisie left-backs — a real, sourced data point, not an inference. Versatility across his career (deployed at right-back, winger, and defensive midfield at various points per multiple sources) suggests a squad-trusted utility player as much as a specialist.
Tenure and leadership are the defining traits of this profile. 11 major honors at PSV: 4 Eredivisie titles (2017-18, 2023-24, 2024-25, 2025-26), 4 Johan Cruyff Shields, 2 KNVB Cups. He's now vice-captain, on a contract through June 2029, with a Transfermarkt value of €18M and a reported (press, unconfirmed) €12M release clause drawing interest from Porto and Leeds United.
