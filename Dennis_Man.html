Data-Driven Scouting Report — Preliminary
Public sources only · Tier 3 not conducted
Dennis Man is a 27-year-old left-footed right winger for PSV Eindhoven and the Romania national team, born 26 August 1998. He began his senior career at UTA Arad in 2015 before moving to FCSB in 2016, where he was named to the Liga I Team of the Year (2018-19), won the Cupa României, and was voted Gazeta Sporturilor's Romanian Footballer of the Year (2020). This report is built entirely from public statistical sources (FotMob, Wikipedia, Transfermarkt) and press coverage — it is not a Tier 3 video-scouted assessment.
His move to Parma in 2020 (€11M) was the highest fee ever paid for a player exported from the Romanian league at the time. Over five seasons there, he made 142 appearances across Serie A and Serie B combined, scoring 28 goals, and helped Parma win the Serie B title in 2023-24. He signed with PSV on 12 August 2025 for a fee reported between €7.5M and €9M, on a four-year contract through 2029.
His 2025-26 debut PSV season shows an elite per-minute output. 7 goals, 7 assists, 1,496 minutes, 7.29 average FotMob rating, only 2 yellow cards — roughly 0.84 goal contributions per 90 minutes, among the best rates in this whole card series. FotMob's own comparison flags him scoring highly on goals, assists, and rating relative to Eredivisie right wingers. The notable open question: his total minutes trail most other PSV attackers covered so far, and public sources don't explain why.
Match-file participation across the supplied set reflects that pattern. He started PSV's season-opening 23 August 2025 win over Groningen (his club debut) and started again against Telstar on 22 March 2026. He was an unused substitute for the 11 April 2026 Sparta Rotterdam match — in the squad, not injured or absent, just not selected to play. The fourth file — Romania vs Netherlands — corresponds to the Euro 2024 Round of 16 tie on 2 July 2024, where Man started for Romania in a 0-3 loss; this is a real, verified match, but a historical one from before his PSV move, kept clearly dated rather than mixed into the current-season set.
His international record is well-established, not a recent development. He made his senior Romania debut in March 2018 and has since earned 43 caps and 11 goals, including a starting role throughout Euro 2024, where Romania topped their group before exiting to the Netherlands.
