Profile

Francesco Camarda is an 18-year-old Italian centre-forward born March 10, 2008 in Milan. 189cm, right-footed. Joined AC Milan's academy at age 7. Still holds the record as the youngest-ever Serie A player (15y 8m) and the youngest Italian to feature in the Champions League (16y, vs Club Brugge). Spent this season on loan at Lecce — 21 Serie A appearances, 1 goal, hampered by a shoulder injury. 4 caps, 4 goals for Italy U21, including a Panenka penalty on his debut. Market value €15M.
