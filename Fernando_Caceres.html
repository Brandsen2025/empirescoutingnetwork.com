Profile

Fernando Gabriel Caceres (born 7 February 1969 in San Isidro, Buenos Aires) is a former Argentine defender who played across 20 seasons in Argentina and Spain. He began his career at Argentinos Juniors, moved to River Plate, and then spent the bulk of his career in La Liga with Real Zaragoza, Valencia, and Celta Vigo — winning the Copa del Rey in 1994 and the UEFA Cup in 1995 with Zaragoza. He later returned to Argentina with Boca Juniors, Independiente, and a second spell at Argentinos Juniors.
