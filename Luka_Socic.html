🌍 Empire FGA Analysis — The Midfield Anchor

Luka Socic represents the archetypal modern defensive midfielder — a player whose value lies not in statistical production but in structural stability and tactical discipline. At 25 years old, he has already established himself as the captain of ND Beltinci, a testament to his leadership qualities and professional maturity. Standing at 1.80m with a left-footed profile, Socic operates primarily as a defensive midfield anchor, shielding the backline and initiating build-up play with conservative, high-percentage passing. His career trajectory — from NS Mura youth product to permanent Beltinci fixture — demonstrates a player comfortable with his role and committed to maximizing his abilities within the Slovenian second tier.
