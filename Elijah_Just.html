Profile

Elijah Henry Just is a 26-year-old New Zealand winger/forward born on 1 May 2000 in Palmerston North, New Zealand. A product of the renowned Olé Football Academy, he began his senior career with Western Suburbs (50 apps, 26 goals) before moving to Eastern Suburbs (20 apps, 7 goals). In 2019, he joined FC Helsingør in Denmark (71 apps, 13 goals across Danish 2nd and 1st Divisions). In 2022, he transferred to AC Horsens (58 apps, 3 goals in Danish Superliga and 1st Division). In his debut season, he scored 7 goals in 35 SPFL apps and was shortlisted for PFA Scotland's Premiership Player of the Year. On the international stage, he has 47 caps and 12 goals for New Zealand, including a historic brace against Iran at the 2026 FIFA World Cup — the first New Zealand player to achieve this feat. He also scored against Belgium in the Round of 16, making him New Zealand's all-time leading World Cup scorer with 3 goals.
