Career Timeline

Born 30 Oct 1960
Born Diego Armando Maradona in Lanús, Buenos Aires.
1976
First team debut at Argentinos Juniors on 20 October 1976 against Talleres de Córdoba (0-1 loss). Played 11 matches, scored 2 goals in the Nacional.
1977–80
Golden boot four consecutive tournaments. Metropolitano '79: 22 goals; Nacional '79: 12; Metropolitano '80: 25; Nacional '80: 18.
1979 · U-20 World Cup
U-20 World Cup winner in Japan. Argentina defeated the Soviet Union 3-1 in the final. César Menotti was the coach.
1981 · Boca Juniors
Transferred to Boca. Debut on 22 February 1981 vs Talleres (4-1 win, scored twice). Won the Metropolitano 1981.
1982–84 · Barcelona
Moved to Barcelona. Won Copa del Rey, Spanish Super Cup, and Copa de Liga (1982/83). Suffered a broken ankle from Goikoetxea.
1984–91 · Napoli
Two Serie A titles (86/87, 88/89), Copa Italia (86/87), UEFA Cup (88/89), Supercoppa Italiana (90/91). The most successful period of his career.
1986 · World Cup
World Cup winner in Mexico. The "Hand of God" and the "Goal of the Century" against England.
1992–93 · Sevilla
Played for Sevilla. Debut on 4 October 1992 vs Athletic Bilbao. Ended with a dispute with Bilardo.
1993 · Newell's Old Boys
Returned to Argentina. Debut on 10 October 1993 vs Independiente (3-1 loss). 5 appearances, 0 goals.
1995–96 · Boca Return
Returned to Boca. Apertura '95: 11 apps, 3 goals; Clausura '96: 13 apps, 2 goals. Apertura '96: 0 apps.

ASSESSED SEASON. 1 appearance — a 3-2 win over Racing. Combined brilliantly with Latorre and Rambert. Preparing for the Apertura.
