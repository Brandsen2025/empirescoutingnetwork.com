🌍 Empire FGA Analysis — The Modern Regista

Leandro Paredes represents the evolution of the Argentine defensive midfielder — a left-footed regista who combines Pirlo-esque passing range with South American combativeness and elite set-piece delivery. His FGA rating of 91 places him in the upper echelon of global midfielders, distinguished by his ability to control tempo from deep positions while maintaining defensive solidity. Unlike traditional ball-winners or pure playmakers, Paredes operates as a hybrid "trequartista" from deep — capable of line-breaking passes, long-range shooting, and tactical fouling when required. His 2022 World Cup triumph with Argentina cemented his status as a generational talent who bridged the gap between defensive responsibility and creative expression.

Under Lionel Scaloni, Paredes has evolved from a deep-lying playmaker into a more mobile "box-to-box" presence, capable of pressing high and recovering second balls. 🌍 Career Journey — From La Bombonera to World Champion

Fifteen-year odyssey across three continents and elite European football.

2010-2014 — Boca Juniors (Youth/Debut)
Product of Boca's famed youth system. Made debut at 16 under Claudio Borghi. Won 2011 Apertura and 2011 Copa Argentina. Established as promising regista with exceptional left-foot technique. Total: 31 apps, 5 goals before European move.
2014 — Chievo Verona
Initial Italian experience. 1 appearance before immediate loan to Roma. Served as stepping stone rather than destination.
2014-2015 — AS Roma
Breakthrough in Serie A. 41 appearances under Rudi García. Developed defensive discipline while maintaining passing range. First taste of Champions League football. Sold to Empoli for playing time.
2015-2016 — Empoli
Revelation season. 34 appearances as deep-lying playmaker in Marco Giampaolo's system. Demonstrated ability to dictate tempo for mid-table side. 2 goals, 4 assists highlighted offensive contribution from deep.
2017-2019 — Zenit St. Petersburg
Established as elite regista. 61 appearances, 8 goals. Won 2018-19 Russian Premier League. Developed long-range shooting and set-piece mastery in less competitive league. €40M transfer to PSG validated status.
2019-2024 — Paris Saint-Germain
Peak club level. 104 appearances across 5 seasons. Won 3 Ligue 1 titles, 3 French Cups. Operated as rotation option behind Verratti but provided crucial balance in big games. Champions League experience against Europe's elite.
2022-2023 — Juventus (Loan)
Return to Serie A. 35 appearances. Reunited with Max Allegri's pragmatic system. Provided set-piece expertise and tempo control for struggling Juve side. 2 goals included screamers from distance.
2023-2024 — AS Roma (Loan)
Second Roma spell. 43 appearances under José Mourinho and Daniele De Rossi. Played Europa League final (lost to Sevilla on penalties). Established as undisputed starter in 3-man midfield.
2025-Present — Boca Juniors
The Return. Signed as designated player/captain after 15 years in Europe. Immediate impact: organizing midfield, mentoring youth, bringing European tactical sophistication to Argentine football. 2025 Copa Libertadores campaign underway.
International Trajectory: Debuted for Argentina in 2017 under Jorge Sampaoli. Missed 2018 World Cup due to injury. Became integral under Scaloni starting 2019 Copa América. Played in 2021 Copa América final vs Brazil (substitute). Started 2022 World Cup final vs France before injury forced substitution (74'). Won 2024 Copa América as squad player. Total: 70 caps, 5 goals.

📋 Empire FGA Scouting Summary:

Leandro Paredes is the archetype of the modern regista — a left-footed deep-lying playmaker who combines Pirlo-esque passing (94) with Argentine combativeness (88 AGR) and elite set-piece delivery (96). His FGA rating of 91 reflects world-class technical ability slightly tempered by consistency issues (82) related to injuries and rotation status at PSG. As a 2022 World Cup winner and three-time Copa América champion (2021, 2024), he has proven his value at the highest level. His return to Boca Juniors as captain at age 31 demonstrates values-based decision making — prioritizing legacy, playing time, and homecoming over financial maximization. For modern scouts, Paredes represents the ideal experienced regista — capable of controlling tempo, defending with intelligence, and delivering in clutch moments. Not a high-energy presser, but a positional master who thinks two steps ahead.

Avoid: Klopp-style high-press systems or counter-attacking teams requiring explosive transitions.
