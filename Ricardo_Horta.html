🌍 Empire FGA Analysis — The Delivery Artist

Ricardo Horta represents the archetype of the modern inverted winger — a prolific crosser with exceptional delivery quality and surprising goal-scoring instincts from wide positions. At 31 years old, he is in his professional prime, combining technical maturity with tactical intelligence developed through years of Portuguese top-flight experience. His FGA rating of 7.4 places him in the Elite Professional tier — a reliable top-tier performer capable of decisive contributions at the highest domestic level. With 94 crosses attempted in just 19 appearances, Horta is among Europe's most prolific suppliers from wide areas, while his 11 goals demonstrate clinical finishing when cutting inside onto his stronger right foot.

🎯 The Delivery Specialist: Horta's crossing volume (94) is extraordinary — averaging nearly 5 crosses per game, a rate comparable to elite Premier League wingers. However, unlike traditional wingers who rely on pace, Horta uses intelligent positioning and technical precision to deliver from deep and advanced positions. His VRP (Vertical Risk Passing) score of 63 reflects his willingness to play penetrative passes into the box rather than safe lateral options. The inverted winger profile suits modern tactical systems perfectly — he hugs the touchline to stretch defenses, then cuts inside to shoot or deliver whipped crosses with his right foot.

🌍 Career Journey — The Portuguese Supply Line

From Setúbal academy to Braga's creative hub and Portugal's squad.

2013-2017 — Vitória Setúbal
Rose through Setúbal academy, making professional debut at 18. Developed crossing technique and wide positioning in traditional Portuguese 4-4-2 systems. 12 goals in 67 games from wide positions attracted attention from Spanish scouts.
2017-2019 — Málaga (Spain)
La Liga experience. Moved to Spain at 23 but struggled for consistent minutes in a relegation-battling side. Learned tactical discipline and defensive tracking required in Spanish football. 2 goals in 24 appearances — disappointing spell but valuable learning experience.
2019-Present — SC Braga (Peak)
The Renaissance. Return to Portugal transformed his career. Under Carlos Carvalhal and subsequent managers, became Braga's primary creative outlet. Three consecutive seasons with 10+ goals. Won 2021 Portuguese Cup and 2024 Portuguese League Cup. Established as one of Primeira Liga's most reliable wingers.
2022-Present — Portugal National Team
Late international bloom. Debut at 28 — called up by Fernando Santos for 2022 World Cup. Used as impact substitute and tactical alternative to faster wingers. Provides crossing variety when Portugal needs aerial threats. Competing with Bernardo Silva and Rafael Leão for wide positions.
The Setúbal Connection: Horta's roots in Vitória Setúbal's famed academy (which produced Luís Figo and Ricardo Quaresma) shaped his technical approach. Unlike the explosive dribblers from Sporting or Porto's academies, Setúbal emphasizes positional discipline and delivery quality. Horta embodies this — less flashy skills, more end product. His Spanish detour, while statistically modest, taught him defensive responsibility that distinguishes him from pure attack-minded wingers in Portugal.
