Profile

One of the more promising products of San Lorenzo's academy in recent years. Plays as a midfielder or winger on either flank, occasionally as an attacking midfielder. Quick but fragile; no top-flight experience of note yet, but those who watched him come through the ranks predict a bright future.

Career to date (as of mid-1997)

Named by José Pekerman in Argentina's preselection for the U-20 squad that went on to win the tournament in Malaysia, but was ultimately left out of the final group. A product of San Lorenzo's own academy; debuted in the Primera División on 17 March 1996 against Racing, in a Copa Libertadores match in which San Lorenzo fielded several youngsters (a 1-0 loss, Carrario scoring), leaving a strong impression on the club's fans. Played two more matches before the regular starters returned once the senior side was eliminated from the Libertadores by River, sending the youth players back to the reserves. Went the entire Apertura '96 tournament without a first-team minute. Starred for the reserve team in the brilliant, championship-winning 1996/97 season — 26 appearances, 3 goals. Mostly a reserve-team player again last season; played just 1 senior match in Clausura '97, with his best form again coming in the reserve championship.
