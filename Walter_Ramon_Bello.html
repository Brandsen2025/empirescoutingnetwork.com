Profile

Walter Ramón Bello (born 5 October 1965 in Rosario, Santa Fe, Argentina) is a former Argentine footballer who played as a forward. He started his professional career at Argentino de Rosario in 1985, then moved to Newell's Old Boys (1986/87 preseason), made his first-division debut in 1988 with San Lorenzo, and had his most documented season in 1989-90 with Instituto de Córdoba, where he scored 10 goals in 29 appearances[reference:0]. After 1990, he played for clubs in Peru (Sport Boys), Mexico (Tecos), and Ecuador (Deportivo Quito)[reference:1][reference:2]. He earned no senior caps for Argentina and later became a football manager[reference:3].
