Profile

A left winger capable of dropping into an enganche or attacking-midfield role. The source credits him with real talent — a powerful shot, good vision, and precise passing — plus the pace to beat defenders one-on-one. It also flags two clear weaknesses for a player his age: excessive individualism and inexperience, both cited as the main obstacles to breaking into Boca's first team.

Career to date (as of mid-1997)

Came through Argentinos Juniors' youth ranks. Boca bought his pass in 1996 along with three other Argentinos Juniors academy products — Juan Román Riquelme, César LaPaglia and Lucas Gatti — betting on the group's future rather than immediate first-team need. Unlike Riquelme, Islas had not broken into senior football by the time of this database snapshot: no professional matches are recorded for him anywhere, including at Boca.
