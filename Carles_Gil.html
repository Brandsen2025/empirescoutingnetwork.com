🌍 Career Journey — Valencia to New England

Spanish technical mastery translated to American soccer through Dutch educational detour.

1992-2011 — Valencia CF Academy
Born in Valencia, Spain — product of the renowned Valencia CF youth academy (La Masia of the South). Developed technical mastery and Spanish possession philosophy from ages 8-19. Won 2011 UEFA U19 Championship with Spain, signaling elite potential.
2011-2014 — Valencia B & First Team
Mestalla breakthrough. Promoted to Valencia first team in 2012 under Mauricio Pellegrino. Made 13 La Liga appearances, showing glimpses of creative talent but struggling for consistent minutes behind established stars. Loaned to Elche (2013-14) for development.
2014-2015 — AZ Alkmaar (Dutch Education)
Total Football immersion. Transferred to AZ Alkmaar in the Eredivisie — the crucible of Total Football. Under John van den Brom, Gil mastered Dutch positional play and spatial interchange. Scored 5 goals in 28 matches, developing the Cruyffian positional flexibility that defines his current style.
2015-2016 — Deportivo La Coruña
Return to La Liga. Signed by Deportivo, making 37 appearances and scoring 3 goals. Showed creative promise but in a struggling team. Period marked by defensive responsibilities that curtailed his attacking freedom.
2019-Present — New England Revolution (MLS)
American Renaissance. Joined Revolution in 2019, initially on loan. 2021 MLS MVP season (4 goals, 18 assists) established him as league's premier playmaker. Current 2025-26 season: 10 goals, 9 assists — maintained elite output into his 30s through tactical intelligence compensating for reduced athleticism.
The Dutch-Spanish Synthesis: Gil's AZ Alkmaar period (2014-15) was the formative tactical experience of his career. The Eredivisie education in positional rotation and spatial exploitation transformed him from a Spanish technical midfielder into a Total Football orchestrator. This Dutch-Spanish hybrid style is perfectly suited to Bruce Arena's system at New England, where fluid interchange between forwards and midfielders is encouraged.
