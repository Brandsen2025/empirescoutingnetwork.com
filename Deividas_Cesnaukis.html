🌍 Career Journey — From Kuršėnai to 5 Nations

Twenty-one-year odyssey across Eastern and Western Europe.

1997-2000 — FK Ekranas
Professional debut. Rose through youth ranks in Panevėžys. Made debut at age 16. 66 appearances, 7 goals as teenage winger. Established as one of Lithuania's brightest young talents, attracting interest from Russian clubs.
2000-2003 — Dynamo Moscow
Major breakthrough. Joined Russian giants at age 19 — a significant step up. 77 appearances, 5 goals across all competitions. Gained valuable experience in competitive Russian Premier League. Learned professional standards at elite Eastern European club.
2004 — Lokomotiv Moscow
Champion. Moved to city rivals and won 2004 Russian Premier League title. Limited playing time (8 league appearances) but earned winner's medal. Tasted top-level success before seeking more playing time abroad.
2005-2009 — Heart of Midlothian
Scottish adventure. Four-year spell in Edinburgh via FBK Kaunas ownership link. 68 appearances, 4 goals. Won 2006 Scottish Cup — scoring in quarter-final vs Partick Thistle. Adapted to physical British football. Injury issues in final seasons limited impact.
2009-2011 — Greek Superleague
Mediterranean transition. One season at Ergotelis (22 apps, 2 goals) followed by move to Aris Thessaloniki (9 apps). Struggled to establish himself in technical Greek football. Beginning of positional shift to right-back.
2011-2014 — FC Baku
Caucasus revival. Three seasons in Azerbaijan. 57 appearances, 8 goals. Won 2011-12 Azerbaijan Cup. Established as right-back/wing-back in 3-5-2 system. Financial stability and regular playing time restored career momentum.
2014-2018 — FK Trakai (Riteriai)
Homecoming. Returned to Lithuania at age 33. Four successful seasons as club captain and right-back. Led team to back-to-back 2nd place finishes (2015-16) and 2016 Lithuanian Cup final. Retired in 2018 as club legend.
Post-Retirement Career: Immediately transitioned to management — appointed Sporting Director of Žalgiris Vilnius (2018-2020) alongside former teammate Deividas Šemberas. Founded DC7 Agency in 2021, becoming football agent representing Lithuanian and Eastern European players. Maintains involvement in Lithuanian football development.
