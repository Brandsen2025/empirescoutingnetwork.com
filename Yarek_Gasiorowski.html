Yarek Gasiorowski Hernandis is a 20-year-old (turns 21 in Jan 2026 cycle) left-footed center-back for PSV Eindhoven, born 12 January 2005 in Spain to a Polish father and Spanish mother. 191cm. FotMob's own positional comparison notes he scores highly on goals, rating, and assists relative to Eredivisie center-backs — a rare combination for a defender in his first season in a new league.
The pedigree behind the move is genuine. He was part of Spain's 2024 UEFA European Under-19 Championship-winning squad (2-0 over France in the Belfast final), started 4 of 5 tournament matches, and was named to the Team of the Tournament specifically for his defensive performances. He's since added 5 Spain U21 caps and 1 goal — a real pathway, though senior recognition hasn't happened yet and shouldn't be assumed.
Match-file participation across the supplied set is mostly positive but not unanimous. He started the 23 August 2025 Groningen match and the 11 April 2026 Sparta Rotterdam match.
