Jim Totime · Empire FGA · Apr 2026
Belliard represents the modern developmental archetype: physically precocious, tactically flexible, technically raw. At 19 years old and 1.89m, he possesses the frame of a defensive midfielder but the movement patterns of a box-to-box player.
His Speed on Action (SOA: 8.0/10) is the standout validated metric — first touch and subsequent action consistently within 0.5 seconds. This enables him to survive in the BGL Ligue despite technical limitations. However, Decision Making (DMA: 1.0) showed critical failure in high-pressure moments — specifically the noted incident vs FC Metz B where "nobody knows if he tried to shoot or cross."
The Character score (CHR: 9.0) is elite — three high-pressure recoveries in first quarter vs Metz B demonstrate leadership through action. Combined with Run Timing (OBI_T: 9.0), he shows instinctive understanding of pressing triggers despite youth.
The incomplete video assessment (5/12 metrics) is the critical blocker. SII_A (explosive acceleration) and SII_R (recovery sprints) remain unverified. OBI_S (space architecture) requires additional matches. Do not commit financially until Tier 3 reaches 10+ confirmed metrics.
Empire FGA Final Verdict
Hold for development or low-cost project signing (€150k–400k). Elite physical/character foundation but significant technical risk. Complete video assessment for OBI_S, SII_A, SII_R before major investment. Ceiling: 6.5–7.0 FGA in compatible pressing system if decision-making improves.
