Profile

Gianluca Scamacca is a 27-year-old Italian centre-forward born January 1, 1999 in Rome. 196cm, right-footed. Youth spells at Lazio, Roma, and PSV before breaking through at Sassuolo (16 league goals, 2021-22). Signed by West Ham for £30.5M plus £5M in 2022 — a move widely seen as underwhelming — before joining Atalanta in 2023, where he's rebuilt his reputation. Contract until June 30, 2027.
