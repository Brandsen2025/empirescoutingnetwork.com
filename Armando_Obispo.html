Profile

A one-club academy product who joined PSV's youth system at age 7 and has remained ever since. Made his Jong PSV debut in August 2016 and his first-team bow in 2017. A season-long loan at Vitesse (2019–20) provided 26 Eredivisie apps of top-flight experience before returning to become a squad-rotation centre-back at PSV. Left-footed and comfortable playing out from the back. His 2025–26 season was defined by a historic international switch to Curaçao and selection for their first-ever World Cup squad.
