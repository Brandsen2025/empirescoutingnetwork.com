🌍 Empire FGA Analysis — The Danish Screen

Silas Andersen is a prototypical modern defensive midfielder, built to provide balance, protection, and simple circulation for a possession-based team. At 21, his profile is defined by his physical presence (1.90m), positional intelligence, and a clear tactical understanding of his screening role. His debut for BK Häcken was a textbook performance: a goal from a set-piece, disciplined defensive work, and seamless integration with creative teammates Gustafson and Rygaard. His FGA rating of 6.60/10 reflects a solid foundation with clear potential to develop into a dominant Allsvenskan midfielder.
