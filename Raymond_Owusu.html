Profile

Raymond Frimpong Owusu is a 24-year-old Ghanaian centre-forward born April 20, 2002 in Kumasi, Ghana. 186cm, right-footed. Product of the Gold Coast Football Academy before moving to Ukrainian club Zorya Luhansk in March 2021[reference:0]. After fleeing Ukraine during the 2022 invasion, he trained with Borussia Dortmund before joining Süper Lig side İstanbulspor in August 2022[reference:1]. Signed for Metalist 1925 Kharkiv in 2023 and is currently on loan at Kudrivka in the Ukrainian Premier League[reference:2][reference:3]. Ghana U20 international.
