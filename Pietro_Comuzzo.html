Profile

Pietro Comuzzo is a 21-year-old Italian centre-back born February 20, 2005 in San Daniele del Friuli — twin brother of amateur footballer Francesco Comuzzo. 188cm, right-footed. Youth spells at Udinese before Fiorentina, where he became a first-team regular. 18 starts in 27 appearances across all competitions this season, though he lost his regular spot as the season wore on. Now on loan at Torino with an option to buy, joined July 29, 2026.
