🏆 Two records, one match
Record Oleg Salenko — only player ever to score 5 goals in a single World Cup finals match. Shared the Golden Boot with Stoichkov (6 goals) despite Russia's group-stage exit — and never played for Russia again.

Record Roger Milla — scored at age 42, becoming (and remaining) the oldest goalscorer in World Cup history.

First 0–0 World Cup final in history — 120 goalless minutes in Los Angeles heat. Decided by the first-ever final shootout:
1 Santos ❌ 1 Baresi ❌ 2 Romário ✅ 2 Albertini ✅ 3 Branco ✅ 3 Evani ✅ 4 Dunga ✅ 4 Massaro ❌ 5 Baggio ❌ — DECIDING MISS
Baggio's shot sailed over the bar — sealing Brazil's 4th title without Brazil needing a 5th kicker. Baggio had carried Italy through the knockout stage (winner vs Nigeria, brace vs Spain, brace vs Bulgaria) but missed the one that mattered most.
