🌍 Empire FGA Analysis — The Greek Sniper

Vangelis Pavlidis represents the archetype of the modern clinical striker — a lethal finisher with exceptional positioning intelligence and ice-cold composure who has rapidly ascended from the Eredivisie to become one of Europe's most efficient goalscorers. His FGA rating of 70 places him in the High Quality tier — combining elite shot technique (STK 8.74) with predatory instincts in the box (RDS 5.37) and surprising aerial threat for his build. Nicknamed "The Greek Sniper" for his precision finishing and ability to strike from any angle, Pavlidis combines traditional target man presence with modern mobility, making him the perfect focal point for Benfica's attacking system. Unlike the pure poachers or static target men, Pavlidis is the complete modern nine — linking play effectively, pressing intelligently, and converting chances at an elite rate (20 goals in 22 games).

⚠️ Tactical Note
Pavlidis operates as the perfect "modern nine" — combining classic hold-up play with intelligent movement. In Schmidt's system at Benfica, he drops deep to link with Di María and Aursnes, then explodes into the box with late runs that exploit the space between center-backs. His partnership with Di María has been telepathic — the Argentine's crosses finding Pavlidis's late runs with surgical precision. Key insight: Pavlidis is most dangerous in transition — when Benfica win the ball high, his ability to spin defenders and finish first-time makes him lethal on the counter. Defensively, he presses intelligently — not with Tevez-like intensity, but with tactical awareness to cut passing lanes.

🌍 Career Journey — From Bochum to Lisbon

Ten-year journey from German lower leagues to Portuguese stardom.

2016-2018 — VfL Bochum II
Rose through VfL Bochum youth system in Germany. Made senior debut in Regionalliga West (4th tier). 12 goals in 38 games showed promise but limited exposure. Developed physical base and finishing technique in the rough German lower leagues.
2018-2021 — Willem II (Breakthrough)
Breakthrough in Netherlands. Moved to Eredivisie for €300K. Developed into prolific scorer — 23 goals in 62 games. Learned movement in the box under Adrie Koster. Won 2020 KNVB Cup (scored in final). Attracted AZ Alkmaar attention.
2021-2024 — AZ Alkmaar (Elite Development)
Transformation into elite striker. 78 goals in 117 games across three seasons. Developed link-up play under Pascal Jansen and Maarten Martens. Europa League experience — scored against Lazio, West Ham, Aston Villa. Became Greece national team starter. Perfected the art of the late run into the box.
2024-Present — SL Benfica (Superstardom)
The Portuguese chapter. Transferred for €18M (now looks like a steal). 20 goals in 22 games in 2025-26 season. Primeira Liga top scorer. Formed devastating partnership with Di María. Champions League goals against Barcelona and Bayern. Now valued at €60M+.
