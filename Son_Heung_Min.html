🌍 Career Journey — Chuncheon to Hollywood

Transcontinental development: Korean foundation, German systematic education, English Premier League stardom, American veteran phase.

1992-2008 — Chuncheon & Seoul Academy
Born in Chuncheon, Gangwon Province. Father Son Woong-jung (ex-footballer) served as primary coach, emphasizing ambidextrous training from age 10. Moved to FC Seoul youth academy at 16, but departed for Europe without making senior debut.
2008-2013 — Hamburger SV (German Foundation)
Bundesliga education. Joined Hamburg at 16. Made Bundesliga debut 2010 (age 18). German systematic development emphasized tactical discipline, pressing, and technical security. Scored 20 goals in 78 appearances. First Korean to win Bundesliga Rookie of the Month (October 2010).
2013-2015 — Bayer Leverkusen (Breakthrough)
Champions League exposure. €10M transfer to Leverkusen. Scored 29 goals in 87 matches. Debut in UEFA Champions League (2014-15), scoring against Benfica and Atletico Madrid. Developed direct running style and counter-attacking excellence in Roger Schmidt's high-tempo system.
2015-2025 — Tottenham Hotspur (Premier League Stardom)
English elite phase. €30M transfer (record for Asian player at time). 2021-22 Premier League Golden Boot (23 goals, shared with Mohamed Salah). Formed legendary "Son-Kane" partnership — most prolific goal combination in Premier League history (47+ goal combinations). Four-time Tottenham Player of the Year. Captain from 2023-2025.
2025-Present — LAFC (MLS Veteran)
American twilight chapter. Free transfer to LAFC following Tottenham contract expiration. Designated Player status. Immediate impact: 9 goals in first 10 MLS matches. Provides veteran leadership for MLS Cup challenge. Transition to player-coach mentorship role anticipated.
The Asian Pioneer Advantage: Son's transcontinental career path created unique tactical versatility: German pressing discipline (Hamburg/Leverkusen), English physical intensity (Tottenham), and Spanish technical influence (Pochettino/Mourinho methodologies). His military service exemption (2018 Asian Games gold) allowed uninterrupted career progression at peak age (26). First Asian player to win Premier League Golden Boot and reach 100+ Premier League goals.
