Profile

Ferran Torres García is a 26-year-old Spanish forward born on 29 February 2000 in Foios, Valencia. A product of the renowned Valencia CF academy, he made his La Liga debut at 17 — becoming the first player born this century to appear in the competition. After 71 apps and 6 goals for Valencia (including Copa del Rey 2019), he moved to Manchester City for €23M in 2020, where he won the Premier League and EFL Cup, scoring 16 goals in 43 apps. In January 2022, Barcelona paid €55M (+€10M add-ons) for his signature — a club-record winter transfer. At Barcelona, he has won 3 La Liga titles (2022-23, 2024-25, 2025-26), Copa del Rey 2024-25, and 3 Supercopa de España. His 2025-26 season was his best La Liga campaign: 33 apps, 16 goals. He was named MVP of the Copa del Rey final vs Real Madrid (April 2025) and finished as the competition's top scorer (6 goals). On the international stage, he has 61 caps and 24 goals for Spain, winning Euro 2024 and finishing as UEFA Nations League top scorer 2020-21. He is currently competing at the 2026 FIFA World Cup.
