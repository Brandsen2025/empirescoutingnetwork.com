Profile

José Rafael Escorcia de la Cruz is a 17-year-old Colombian left winger born on July 4, 2009. 176cm, right-footed. Product of the Atlético Nacional youth academy, nicknamed "Vini" for his Vinicius Jr-like dribbling style. Breakout tournament at the Sudamericano Sub-17 2026 where he was top scorer with 4 goals and 2 assists, including a brace vs Brazil in the semi-final and a goal in the 4-0 final win over Argentina. Also represented Colombia at the FIFA U-17 World Cup 2025 in Qatar (3 starts, 4 appearances). Idol: Luis Díaz. Currently transitioning to Nacional U20 for the 2026 season. Right-footed playing predominantly on the left wing, enabling inside cuts and diagonal dribbling.
