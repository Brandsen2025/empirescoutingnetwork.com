Profile

A 6ft 4in Dutch goalkeeper with over 200 senior appearances and a trophy cabinet including 2 Eredivisie titles, 2 KNVB Cups, and 4 Johan Cruyff Shields. On loan at Sparta Rotterdam from PSV for 2025–26, where he has established himself as the Eredivisie leader in clean sheets (11). Known for exceptional distribution — 94% long-ball accuracy, 370 successful long passes (1st in league). Previously saved the decisive penalty in the 2023 KNVB Cup final vs Ajax. Netherlands U19–U21 international, senior squad call-up in 2020.
