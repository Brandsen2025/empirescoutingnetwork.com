Profile

Moise Kean (full name Bioty Moise Kean) is a 26-year-old Italian centre-forward born February 28, 2000 in Vercelli. 183cm, right-footed. Won the Serie A title in his Juventus debut season (2016-17) as a teenager. Has since played for six different clubs or loan spells: Hellas Verona, Everton, PSG, back to Juventus, and now Fiorentina since July 2024 (€13M, contract to 2029). Market value €45M. Posted 24 caps and 11 goals for Italy, including the opener in the 2026 World Cup playoff final.
