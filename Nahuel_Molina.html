🌍 Empire FGA Analysis — The Modern Wing-Back

Nahuel Molina represents the evolution of the modern fullback — a defensive foundation with attacking enterprise that has become essential in contemporary football. His FGA rating of 88 places him among the elite tier of modern right-backs, combining Simeone's defensive discipline with Scaloni's attacking freedom. From his Barça Juniors Luján beginnings to World Cup glory with Argentina, Molina has demonstrated remarkable adaptability across multiple leagues and tactical systems. His 2022 World Cup-winning goal against Netherlands epitomizes his clutch mentality and technical quality under pressure.

🌍 Career Journey — From Barça Academy to World Champion

Nine-year ascent from provincial Argentina to World Cup glory in Qatar.

2012-2018 — Boca Juniors Academy
Started at Barça Juniors Luján (Barcelona academy project in Argentina) before integration into Boca's youth ranks in 2012. Made first team debut 18 Feb 2016 vs San Martín (full 90 minutes at 17 years old). Only 8 first-team appearances before loan moves. Part of generation including Benedetto and Pavón.
2018 — Defensa y Justicia (Loan)
Breakthrough. 17 appearances, 1 goal. Established as reliable right-back in Argentine top flight. Learned defensive discipline under Ariel Holan system.
2019-2020 — Rosario Central (Loan)
22 appearances, 1 goal. Continued development at one of Argentina's historic clubs. Gained experience in high-pressure derby matches.
2020-2022 — Udinese (Serie A)
European Education. 64 appearances, 9 goals. Transformed into attacking fullback. Developed crossing and offensive positioning under Italian tactical discipline. Free transfer to 2025 contract.
2022-Present — Atlético Madrid
Elite Level. 108 appearances, 6 goals. €10M + €5M variables transfer. First-choice right-back under Simeone. Won 2022-23 La Liga third place, consistent Champions League performer. Contract until 2027.
The 2022 World Cup Goal: 9 Dec 2022, Lusail Stadium — Molina's finest moment. Messi's through ball found Molina's underlapping run; composed finish past Noppert opened scoring vs Netherlands in quarterfinal. Goal epitomized his attacking quality and big-game mentality. Argentina went on to win on penalties, eventually lifting the trophy.
