Profile

Juan Pablo Sorín is a 21-year-old Argentine left-back born on 5 May 1976 in Capital Federal, Argentina. 176cm, 68kg. A U-20 World Cup winner (Qatar '95) and Copa Libertadores winner (1996), Sorín is technically excellent, disciplined, and a surprisingly effective aerial player despite his height. Passarella's protégé, he was capped at senior level during the 1998 World Cup qualifiers.
