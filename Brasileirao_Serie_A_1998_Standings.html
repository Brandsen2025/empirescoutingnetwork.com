Do not use this page to infer, estimate, or extrapolate any individual player's numbers.
ℹ️ Why this exists. Built after checking whether 1998 Brasileirão could support a real player-stat league build (it can't, at any legitimate free-data quality) and after ruling out Championship Manager 3 1998-99 as a source — its player ratings are one researcher's 30-year-old subjective 1-20 opinions, not measured data, and extracting its database would raise real IP concerns. This table is the honest alternative: real final standings, properly sourced, clearly scoped.

Top 8 advanced to a championship playoff (quarterfinal → semifinal → final, each a best-of-three-legs mini-series). Corinthians won the title by taking the final's decisive third leg 2-0 (Dec 23) after the first two legs were drawn 2-2 and 1-1 with Cruzeiro. Grêmio — Ronaldinho Gaúcho's club that season — finished 8th in the regular table and was eliminated by Corinthians in the quarterfinal, 2 legs to 1 (Corinthians won legs 1 and 3, 1-0 each; Grêmio won leg 2, 2-0). Top scorer: Viola (Santos), 21 goals.
