🌍 Empire FGA Analysis — The Korean Playmaker

Lee Hyun-ju represents the new wave of East Asian technical midfielders combining Korean academy discipline with German tactical education and Portuguese creative flair. The 22-year-old attacking midfielder became FC Arouca's club-record signing (€1.5M) in July 2025, surpassing the fee paid for compatriot Taichi Fukui. A product of the famed Pohang Steelers academy and Bayern Munich's development system, Lee brings elite technical training and international experience (senior debut for South Korea November 2024) to Portugal's Primeira Liga.

Tree Mers FC → Pohang Steelers → Bayern Munich II → Wehen Wiesbaden → Hannover 96 → 🟡 FC Arouca
🔴 Bayern Munich Pedigree: Lee spent 3.5 years in the Bayern Munich system (2022-2025), initially joining on loan before making the move permanent in August 2022. Despite never breaking into the first team, he developed significantly with Bayern II in Regionalliga Bayern (26 apps, 10 goals, 3 assists) and gained valuable 2. Bundesliga experience through loans at Wehen Wiesbaden (31 apps, 4 goals) and Hannover 96 (24 apps, 3 goals). His idols Joshua Kimmich and Jamal Musiala influenced his development as a technically gifted, versatile midfielder.
