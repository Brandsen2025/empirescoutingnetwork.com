Data-Driven Scouting Report — Preliminary
Public sources only · Tier 3 not conducted
Sergiño Gianni Dest is a 25-year-old right-back for PSV Eindhoven and the USA national team, born 3 November 2000 in Almere, Netherlands, to an American father. 173cm, right-footed, wears No. 21. He came through Ajax's academy, debuted for the first team in July 2019, won Ajax's Talent of the Year award and made the 2020 Golden Boy shortlist before a €21M transfer to Barcelona in October 2020, where he won the Copa del Rey. This report is built entirely from public statistical sources (FotMob, Wikipedia, Transfermarkt) and press coverage — it is not a Tier 3 video-scouted assessment.
Two serious injuries have shaped his career arc since leaving Barcelona. A hamstring injury during a 2022-23 AC Milan loan required surgery and ended that season early. A torn ACL in April 2024 — sustained during a PSV loan spell that was otherwise integral to their 2023-24 Eredivisie title — cost him the rest of that campaign and the entire 2024 Copa América. He didn't return to match action until 8 March 2025, and was deliberately left off the 2025 Concacaf Gold Cup roster so he could continue an individualized rebuilding program rather than rush back into tournament football.
2025-26 is his first full, healthy PSV season since the injury. 2,204 minutes, 1 goal, 5 assists, 7.42 average FotMob rating, 5 yellow cards. He moved to PSV permanently (free, Barcelona retaining a resale percentage) in June 2024, on a contract through 2028, with a current Transfermarkt value of €18M.
Confirmed match involvement across the supplied files is mixed, and the exclusions matter. He started the 23 August 2025 Groningen match. He was unavailable — injured — for both the 22 March 2026 Telstar match and the 11 April 2026 Sparta Rotterdam match, so those two files are left off this card per instruction. On the international side, he was not part of the January 2025 USA training-camp friendlies vs Venezuela and Costa Rica (an MLS-only domestic camp he wasn't eligible for as a Europe-based player, and was still injured regardless), and was explicitly left off the June 2025 Gold Cup roster vs Trinidad and Tobago for rehab reasons — all three of those files are excluded. He did feature in the two USA files that remain: he scored the opening goal in the 31 May 2026 Senegal friendly, and started the 19 June 2026 World Cup Group D opener against Australia.
One further honest note on temperament: in a November 2023 Nations League qualifier vs Trinidad and Tobago (a different, earlier match than the 2025 Gold Cup fixture), Dest picked up two bookings and a red card for reacting to a referee decision, and publicly apologized afterward, with his coach at the time citing it as a maturing moment. Worth including as a real, sourced data point rather than pretending the incident didn't happen or treating it as still current three seasons later.
