Data-Driven Scouting Report — Preliminary
Public sources only · Tier 3 not conducted
Matěj Kovář is a 26-year-old Czech goalkeeper for PSV Eindhoven, born 17 May 2000. He came through Czech side 1.FC Slovácko before joining Manchester United's academy in January 2018, spending nearly six years there including loan spells at Swindon Town (senior debut), Burton Albion, and Sparta Prague, where he won the Czech league title. A £7.7M move to Bayer Leverkusen followed in August 2023, and he joined PSV on loan on 15 July 2025 before PSV made the deal permanent on 13 January 2026, signing him through June 2030.
He was PSV's undisputed #1 all season — 31 Eredivisie appearances plus 8 Champions League matches, total ever-presence. His save percentage (67.5% domestically, 66.0% in Europe) is respectable and roughly consistent across competitions.
But the clean-sheet number deserves real scrutiny. 7 clean sheets in 31 Eredivisie matches — a 23% rate, with 40 goals conceded and a 1.29 goals-against-per-90 — is not the profile of an elite title-winning #1. That could reflect PSV's own attacking, higher-line defensive approach rather than individual shot-stopping failure, but it's not something to wave away just because the team won the league. Empire's job is to separate "his team won" from "he had a great individual season," and on the public numbers alone, that gap isn't fully closed.
