🌍 Career Journey — From Prodigy to Resilience

German development: Hamburg academy excellence, Bayern Munich crucible, and Danish redemption.

2000 — Born: Bad Segeberg, Germany
Born in Bad Segeberg, Schleswig-Holstein. Early football education at SV Wahlstedt (age 4-10) before joining Hamburger SV academy at age 10. Developed through HSV youth system emphasizing technical security and traditional German striking.
2016-2017 — HSV Youth Sensation
Breakthrough season. Scored 26 goals in 21 U17 appearances, becoming top scorer in North/Northeast group. Awarded Fritz Walter Medal Gold (2017) as Germany's best U17 player. 37 goals in 45 total youth appearances established him as country's premier striking talent.
2017-2019 — Bundesliga Debut & Bayern Transfer
Professional emergence. Became first Bundesliga goalscorer born in the 2000s (October 2017 vs Hertha BSC, aged 17). Scored 3 goals in 35 Bundesliga/2. Bundesliga appearances for HSV. February 2019: Signed for Bayern Munich in high-profile transfer with flexible start date (ultimately July 2019).
2019-2022 — Bayern Munich Nightmare
Career crucible. Joined Bayern Munich but never made a Bundesliga appearance for first team. Suffered scaphoid bone fracture (Sept 2019) and radial bone injury (Nov 2019), sidelining him for months. Made only 1 DFB-Pokal appearance for first team; 42 appearances (8 goals) for Bayern II in 3. Liga. Psychological test: Faced media scrutiny as "Bayern flop" while younger players (Zirkzee, Musiala) surpassed him.
2022-2025 — Holstein Kiel Rebuilding
Homecoming and redemption. Joined Holstein Kiel permanently (July 2022) after loan spell. 2023-24: Scored 5 goals in 17 appearances as Kiel achieved promotion to Bundesliga. 2024-25: Made 23 Bundesliga appearances (2 goals) before Kiel were relegated back to 2. Bundesliga. Despite limited output, rebuilt match fitness and confidence.
Note: The 2024-25 Bundesliga season concluded in May 2025. Holstein Kiel finished in the relegation zone and returned to 2. Bundesliga for the 2025-26 season. Arp subsequently transferred to Odense BK in July 2025.
2025-Present — Danish Renaissance
Career revival. Joined Odense Boldklub (OB) in Danish Superliga (July 2025). Immediate impact: 8 goals, 3 assists in 18 appearances (2025-26). Average FotMob rating 7.52 — elite for league. Goal every 190 minutes demonstrates return to clinical finishing. Contract until 2028 suggests long-term commitment to revival.
The Resilience Advantage: Arp's Bayern Munich experience, while statistically barren, created a unique psychological profile. His Character (CHR) score of 8.8 reflects trauma recovery — the ability to rebuild confidence after elite-level failure is rare. In the Empire FGA database, only 12% of players who fail at elite clubs recover to post 8.0+ FGA ratings. His Consistency (CON: 10.0) suggests mental stability independent of match pressure.
