Jim Totime · Empire FGA · Feb 2026
Cedric Hatenboer represents the Anderlecht loanee prototype — acquired young, loaned for developmental minutes, monitored for progression. A 20-year-old two-footed defensive midfielder with elite sprint speed (SPR 8.10) and newly discovered ghost run ability (OBI_T 8.33/10) from video assessment.
His DMA composite (8.75/10) reflects excellent decision-making in buildup phases. The 7 chances created (1.28/90) and 67% opposition half passing demonstrate playmaking upside rare for a developmental #6. However, 47% duel success and 1.7/10 interception rate expose defensive technical gaps.
The critical concern is the disciplinary pattern: 2 red cards in 135 minutes vs Ajax, both in second halves when fitness wanes. The 6 substitutions in 7 starts suggests stamina limitations contributing to mental fatigue. 5 of 12 Tier 3 video metrics remain pending — full assessment requires 2+ additional matches.
Empire FGA Final Verdict
Hold for 2026-27. Complete Tier 3 video assessment, address fitness/stamina, monitor disciplinary record. If resolved, projects as Belgian Pro League squad player by 2027. If pattern persists, value declines to €200-400k (loan/transfer risk).
